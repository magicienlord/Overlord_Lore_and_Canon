# OVERLORD Franchise Locations and Geography Reference

Status: PRIMARY-SOURCE SYNTHESIS

Date: 2026-09-11

Authority: franchise reference only. These are historical/source locations from the original games. They are not automatically present, named, or geographically preserved in OVERLORD REIGN.

## Geographic discipline

The supplied games provide strong local and regional relationships but do not provide a complete surveyed world map with absolute distances.

Therefore this file distinguishes:

- `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT LOCATION`: named directly in dialogue/system text;
- `PRIMARY-IMPLEMENTATION`: map/domain organization from `.omp` and system map names;
- `RELATIVE`: connected or associated in game progression, without claiming real-world scale or cardinal precision beyond the source.

## Overlord I regions

### The Dark Tower

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT LOCATION`.

The Tower is the Overlord's seat of power and contains/hosts major facilities including the throne room, spawning infrastructure, dungeon, forge, private quarters, Tower Heart functions, and Gates.

The Tower is damaged and depleted when the new Overlord awakens and is rebuilt through recovered resources and artifacts.

This is the historical Tower of Overlord I. OVERLORD REIGN's present Dark Tower is already established as a physically new Tower and should not be treated as the surviving same structure.

### Mellow Hills and Spree

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT LOCATION`.

The Mellow Hills are the first major human/Halfling region and later host the Mellow Hills Abyss in Raising Hell.

Spree is a human village within this regional context. Source material connects it with farms, refugees, local guards, food shortages, Castle Spree, and conflict with Halflings.

The Halfling domain includes a village, work-camp/slave-camp areas, Halfling homes, and Melvin's kitchen complex.

### Evernight Forest

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT LOCATION`.

Evernight Forest is an Elven region described as once filled with Elven magic. Its corruption is tied to Oberon, nightmares, poisonous growth, and the decline of the local Elven community.

Associated source locations include the Viridian Caverns, home of Green Minions, and an Elven/Mother Goddess temple area.

Raising Hell later manifests an Evernight-related Abyss.

### Heaven's Peak

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT LOCATION`.

Heaven's Peak is a city dominating swamp-laden surrounding lands.

Its major source locations include:

- the city itself;
- Angelis Keep / the Citadel;
- sewers and Understreets;
- the Halfway to Heaven Inn;
- plague-affected districts;
- religious/Silent Order infrastructure;
- the Moist Hollows, associated with Blue Minions.

The city is placed under lockdown during the plague crisis.

### Golden Hills

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT LOCATION`.

The Golden Hills are identified as home to the Dwarves.

Associated locations include:

- gold-mining infrastructure;
- the Brewery running through the mountain;
- Royal Halls;
- Arcanium mine;
- quarry/mines;
- temple-construction site built with enslaved Elven labor.

Raising Hell later manifests a Golden Hills / Dwarven Abyss.

### Ruborian Desert

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT LOCATION`.

Ruboria is a dry desert region characterized in source dialogue by severe sand and sandstorms.

The Overlord reaches it through a secluded Gate while pursuing Jewel. Jewel's hideout/domain and later Kahn-related events are associated with the Ruborian campaign material.

Raising Hell later records another Abyss manifestation in the Ruborian Desert.

## Raising Hell locations

### Evil Abysses

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT LOCATION TYPE`.

Abysses appear in or are connected to several established regions and are controlled through Abyss Stones.

They are supernatural spaces rather than ordinary extensions of surface geography.

Known campaign associations include Mellow Hills, Evernight, Golden Hills/Dwarven material, Heaven's Peak-related material, and Ruboria.

### Infernal Abyss

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT LOCATION`.

The Infernal Abyss contains the Temple of the Forgotten God and becomes the final Raising Hell location.

The Overlord I / Raising Hell ruler is trapped/lost there at the conclusion.

The source treats the Infernal Abyss as distinct in name and role from the later Netherworld.

Its exact cosmological position relative to the Netherworld remains `UNKNOWN`.

## Overlord II regions

### Netherworld

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT LOCATION / REALM`.

The Netherworld becomes the Overlord II player's seat of power after it accepts him as its true Master.

It contains/builds infrastructure including Gates, Graveyard, Barracks/Minion facilities, private quarters, magic-related facilities, and the Netherworld Tower/domain.

Natural laws are described as flexible there.

It served as a Minion refuge during the Exodus after the previous Master was lost.

### Nordberg

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT LOCATION`.

Nordberg is a northern human settlement/region and the childhood setting of the Overlord II player.

The source includes:

- Nordberg Town;
- Town Hall / governmental control objectives;
- surrounding wilderness;
- Midwinter customs;
- Empire occupation/governance;
- an Elf commune nearby;
- a Nordberg Sanctuary;
- the player's remembered foundling/witch-boy history.

Nordberg can undergo materially different states depending on the Overlord's method of conquest.

### Hunting Grounds

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT LOCATION`.

A region used by Empire hunters and rich in Lifeforce-bearing creatures and people.

It also introduces Florian Greenheart and the Soldiers of the Sanctuary and acts as a route toward Sanctuary-related objectives.

### Everlight

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT LOCATION`.

Everlight is described as an ancient Elven retreat and remains culturally associated with Elves, tourism, the Mother Goddess, magic, and natural life.

Source material distinguishes multiple Everlight areas, including jungle, gates, facility, temple, town, and associated coastal/island movement.

Everlight is not a purely isolated sacred enclave. Ambient dialogue shows tourism, commerce, local residents, and conflict with Empire visitors and forces.

### Sanctuaries

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT LOCATION TYPE`.

Sanctuaries are protected places for magical/natural beings.

Named/source-distinguished Sanctuary locations include the Nordberg Sanctuary and the Last Sanctuary.

The Last Sanctuary is specifically described as a safe place for creatures of Light magic and is closely connected with Queen Fay.

Sanctuaries should not be conflated with the Netherworld or Abysses.

### Wastelands

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT LOCATION`.

The Wastelands are the devastated remains associated with the Great Cataclysm and the previous Minion/Tower homeland.

Source text describes:

- ruined/cataclysmic terrain;
- soured magic and magical ooze;
- Tower Heart shards scattered there;
- active Gates;
- Gnome communities;
- Sanctuary-related routes/events;
- the ruins of the old Dark Tower context.

The Wastelands are therefore a physically devastated region in Overlord II source continuity, not merely an abstract magical dimension.

OVERLORD REIGN's later decision that the Cataclysm also produced a dimensional rupture associated with Minecraft's End is a REIGN adaptation layered onto this source fact.

### Empire territory and Empire City

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT LOCATION`.

Empire City is the political/metropolitan center of the Glorious Empire and is regarded by provincial NPCs as a major, wealthy, prestigious destination.

Source locations within or around the Empire campaign include:

- Empire Harbor;
- Empire City districts and slums;
- the Arena;
- Heartlands/camps;
- Imperial Palace;
- anti-magic defenses;
- final-battle/administrative spaces tied to Solarius.

The Empire has sufficient territorial reach to govern/occupy settlements such as Nordberg and exert pressure in Everlight and Sanctuary regions.

## Relative historical geography

The games establish meaningful regional travel and connections, but the current corpus does not justify drawing a precise continental map from dialogue alone.

Safe source-level statements include:

- Spree and Mellow Hills belong to the same campaign region;
- Evernight is a distinct forest/Elven region;
- Heaven's Peak dominates surrounding swamp-laden lands;
- Golden Hills are a Dwarven mountain/mining region;
- Ruboria is a distinct desert region;
- Nordberg, Everlight, Wastelands, and Empire territories are distinct Overlord II regions connected by Gates, ships, roads, or campaign transitions;
- Netherworld and Abysses are supernatural realms/spaces accessed through magical Gates rather than ordinary overland travel.

Anything more cartographically exact requires map-file reconstruction or visual/gameplay evidence.

## OVERLORD REIGN adoption rule

Status: EXISTING PROJECT CONSTRAINT.

OVERLORD REIGN does not recreate the original game maps and has not automatically adopted these historical locations as current settlements.

Their primary value is:

- historical reference;
- architectural/cultural precedent;
- quest memory and ruins if explicitly adopted later;
- names and stories known to long-lived characters such as Gnarl;
- evidence for how the franchise connects regions and supernatural realms.

A procedural Minecraft settlement should not be renamed `Spree`, `Everlight`, `Nordberg`, or another franchise place merely because it resembles the source.

If any historical location survives, is rediscovered, has descendants, or leaves ruins in REIGN, that must be an explicit project decision.
