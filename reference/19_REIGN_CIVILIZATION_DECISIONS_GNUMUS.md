# OVERLORD REIGN Gnumu Civilization Decisions

Status: PROJECT DECISION MIRROR

Date: 2026-09-11

Authority: mirrors explicit OVERLORD REIGN decisions. Numbered project canon files remain authoritative.

## Origin

Status: CANON / REIGN ADAPTATION

The Gnumus of the present quest region descend from Halflings altered over centuries by misuse of Gluttony magic.

The transformation is regional, not universal. Ordinary Halflings still exist elsewhere in the world.

The affected Halfling population gradually became the present Gnumus over generations.

The truth of that ancestry has been lost even to the Gnumus themselves. Their current culture does not need to preserve conscious Halfling historical identity.

This means the Gnumu culture can remain primarily grounded in the installed Gnumus mod's own worker, merchant, shaman, hunter, settlement, farming, camp, and local social behavior rather than artificially preserving every old Halfling custom.

## Quest Anchor and Starter

Status: CANON DESIGN / PLANNED IMPLEMENTATION

The civilization quest anchor is the principal Large Gnumus Settlement or equivalent main Gnumu settlement selected for the canonical world.

The civilization main quest begins through one designated Elder Shaman.

Elder Shaman is a quest/political role layered onto a suitable existing Gnumu shaman entity or NPC rather than a requirement for a new species-wide class.

Ordinary Gnumu settlements, random player actions, or interaction with unrelated Gnumus must not automatically start the civilization arc.

## Historical Knowledge

Status: CANON

The Gnumus themselves do not know that they descend from Halflings.

Their old regional Halfling ancestry may therefore be revealed externally through old records, magical evidence, Gnarl's interpretation, ruins, or another authored quest device if the questline ever needs that fact exposed.

It must not be treated as common Gnumu cultural knowledge.

## Disposition and Rewards

Status: CANON DESIGN / PLANNED IMPLEMENTATION

No special civilization-wide hostility is required solely because of their ancestry.

The low-overhead subjugation/reward scope is sufficient:

- merchant benefits;
- access to Shaman services or quest rewards;
- tribute;
- safe settlement use;
- civilization-specific sidequest access.

Hunters remain settlement defenders unless existing mechanics provide additional behavior without requiring a new persistent military simulation.

## Destruction

Status: CANON DESIGN / PLANNED IMPLEMENTATION

Destruction applies to the main Gnumu quest anchor as a local authored outcome.

Destroying that settlement does not imply global extinction of the Gnumu people or of Halflings elsewhere in the world.
