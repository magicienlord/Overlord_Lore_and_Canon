# OVERLORD REIGN Campaign Structure Decisions

Status: PROJECT DECISION MIRROR

Date: 2026-09-11

Authority: mirrors explicit OVERLORD REIGN decisions. Numbered project canon files remain authoritative.

## Campaign openness

Status: CANON DESIGN / PLANNED IMPLEMENTATION

After the opening and early Tower/Minion recovery phase, the campaign should become semi-open rather than strictly linear.

Gnarl may recommend priorities, but several major arcs may be pursued concurrently.

Hard sequencing locks should exist only where lore, world state, or actual mod mechanics require them.

The quest system must not impose an artificial corridor structure on ordinary Minecraft exploration.

## Civilization arcs are independent from central campaign completion

Status: CANON CAMPAIGN RULE

The central main campaign is independent from civilization disposition resolution.

All ten disposition civilizations may receive substantial authored quest arcs, but resolving all ten is not required to complete the central campaign.

A civilization may remain unresolved, neutral, hostile, subjugated, destroyed locally, or otherwise in its authored state without blocking the central story unless a specific central quest explicitly depends on a local fact for a source-supported reason.

Civilization arcs remain major content rather than disposable filler, but they are parallel political/world content rather than a checklist required for the main ending.

## Native mod progression handoff

Status: CANON DESIGN / PLANNED IMPLEMENTATION

When an installed mod already provides a meaningful progression loop, boss sequence, questline, dungeon progression, or specialist mechanic, OVERLORD REIGN should preserve that native loop wherever technically practical.

Overlord Quests should:

- frame the native content through Gnarl and REIGN context;
- point the player toward the native progression when narratively appropriate;
- track relevant milestones and outcomes;
- react to completion or important choices;
- use the resulting state as input to later REIGN quests where appropriate.

Overlord Quests should not unnecessarily replace native progression merely to make it appear inside its own UI.

## Sequence breaking and prior accomplishment recognition

Status: CANON DESIGN / PLANNED IMPLEMENTATION

Minecraft exploration may cause the player to complete content before Gnarl or the quest log formally directs them to it.

The quest system should recognize already-accomplished facts whenever technically possible.

Examples include:

- killing a boss early;
- finding a quest anchor early;
- completing a dungeon early;
- obtaining a key item early;
- completing a native mod progression milestone before REIGN formally points toward it.

The system must not respawn or duplicate a completed boss or world event merely because the corresponding main quest was not yet active.

Gnarl and the quest record may react to the Overlord having acted without instruction.

Sequence breaking should produce adapted state and dialogue rather than invalidating legitimate Minecraft play.

## Quest-critical structures exist in the world before activation

Status: CANON WORLD / IMPLEMENTATION RULE

Quest-critical structures and civilization anchors should exist normally in the generated or authored world rather than materializing only when a quest activates.

The player may discover locations such as a Bastille, Grove, Dwarven hold, civilization settlement, dungeon, or other anchor before its formal campaign introduction.

Discovery alone does not automatically resolve political or narrative state.

A quest begins or advances only when its actual authored conditions are satisfied.

## Persistent world after the main ending

Status: CANON CAMPAIGN RULE

Completing the central campaign does not end the Minecraft world.

The post-ending world remains fully playable, analogous to Minecraft continuing after its own End sequence.

After the central ending, the player may continue:

- unresolved civilization arcs;
- sidequests;
- tribute and services;
- Tower development;
- exploration;
- native mod progression;
- post-campaign authored content;
- ordinary sandbox play.

The Overlord's reign continues beyond the formal conclusion of the central campaign.

## No campaign soft-locks from destructive choices

Status: CANON DESIGN / PLANNED IMPLEMENTATION

Authored choices may permanently destroy NPCs, settlements, services, quest branches, rewards, alliances, or local opportunities.

However, no ordinary destructive choice should make the overall central campaign impossible to finish.

If the player destroys something that would otherwise have provided a route forward, the campaign should provide an alternate, usually harsher or less convenient path, where technically and narratively feasible.

Permanent consequences are encouraged. Hidden main-campaign dead ends are not.

## Reign outcome without a corruption meter

Status: CANON DESIGN / PLANNED IMPLEMENTATION

OVERLORD REIGN must not reintroduce a global numeric corruption, morality, domination, destruction, friendship, or reputation meter.

Major choices write explicit outcome markers describing what actually happened.

The central campaign and later content may react to the pattern of those explicit outcomes through:

- Gnarl commentary;
- branch availability;
- ending or epilogue-style acknowledgements;
- persistent world state;
- services or losses;
- later quest prerequisites;
- optional post-campaign material.

The system should reason from authored facts rather than convert them into a single hidden score.

## Spoiler firewall for campaign development

Status: DESIGN WORKFLOW RULE

The Overlord wants the detailed campaign to remain surprising to a meaningful degree.

Therefore campaign interview and approval should remain focused on player-facing structural rules, technical constraints, required lore boundaries, and broad gameplay expectations.

Do not expose detailed quest twists, betrayals, boss framing, reveal timing, branch traps, dialogue payoffs, or surprise outcomes during ordinary planning unless the Overlord explicitly asks to review them.

Detailed quest construction may proceed behind this spoiler firewall using the established canon, source corpus, mod mechanics, and approved campaign structure.
