# OVERLORD REIGN Mod Questline Assignments

Status: PROJECT DECISION MIRROR / INTERVIEW IN PROGRESS

Date: 2026-09-13

Authority: records explicit Overlord decisions about which installed or planned mods deserve dedicated questlines, supporting integration, popup acknowledgement, or no questline. This file complements `32_REIGN_QUESTLINE_COVERAGE_LEDGER.md` and will be finalized when the interview is complete.

## Assignment categories

- FULL QUESTLINE: dedicated authored progression with a meaningful beginning, development, and climax or conclusion.
- NATIVE PROGRESSION QUESTLINE: dedicated OVERLORD REIGN framing around a mod's existing progression rather than replacing its mechanics.
- SMALL ARC: finite authored side arc or tutorial-sized chain.
- ABSORBED IN OTHER QUESTLINE: acknowledged through another established questline rather than given a separate one.
- POPUP ACKNOWLEDGEMENT: character popup commentary or contextual recognition only, with no dedicated quest progression.
- NO DEDICATED QUESTLINE: usable as ambient world content, rewards, encounters, structures, or mechanics without its own authored chain.
- PENDING: not yet decided.

# Confirmed acknowledgement-only / non-questline assignments

## Darker Depths

Assignment: POPUP ACKNOWLEDGEMENT / NO DEDICATED QUESTLINE

Decision: despite substantial underground content and possible mechanical endpoints, it should not receive its own questline. This prevents questline inflation across the modpack.

Popup NPCs may acknowledge noteworthy discoveries, structures, enemies, or progression where appropriate.

## Born in Chaos

Assignment: POPUP ACKNOWLEDGEMENT / NO DEDICATED QUESTLINE

Decision: despite multiple bosses and an advancement structure, it should not receive a dedicated questline.

Selected bosses, encounters, or achievements may receive contextual popup acknowledgement from Gnarl or other appropriate non-corporeal personnel.

## Realm RPG: Imps & Demons

Assignment: POPUP ACKNOWLEDGEMENT / NO DEDICATED QUESTLINE

Decision: demonic content does not warrant a standalone questline. It remains outside the generalized civilization disposition system and may receive localized Gnarl or other popup commentary.

## Mythic Mounts / Companions! / Tameable Beasts / Domestication Innovation

Assignment: POPUP ACKNOWLEDGEMENT / NO DEDICATED QUESTLINE

Decision: do not create a synthetic Beastmaster or Menagerie questline merely to unify these mods. Appropriate acquisitions, taming events, rare creatures, or milestones may receive popup acknowledgement.

## Artifacts / Relics

Assignment: POPUP ACKNOWLEDGEMENT / NO DEDICATED QUESTLINE

Decision: use as treasure, equipment, rewards, discoveries, or quest objects where useful. Significant finds may receive popup acknowledgement, but there is no dedicated collection questline.

## Farmer's Delight / Dungeon's Delight / L_Ender's Delight

Assignment: POPUP ACKNOWLEDGEMENT / ABSORBED IN OTHER QUESTLINES

Decision: no separate cooking questline.

The established Gluttony / Farmer's Spell questline is the dedicated food-magic arc. Other food mods support existing questlines, dungeons, civilization content, Cataclysm content, or general play and may receive popup acknowledgement.

## Shield Expansion / Fantasy Weapons / Fantasy Armor and similar general equipment systems

Assignment: POPUP ACKNOWLEDGEMENT / NO DEDICATED QUESTLINE

Decision: these provide equipment, rewards, specialist-provider material, and ordinary progression but do not warrant independent questlines. Popup acknowledgement may be used selectively where a discovery or acquisition is noteworthy.

## Hot Iron

Assignment: ABSORBED IN TOWER RESTORATION QUESTLINE

Decision: Hot Iron must be explicitly acknowledged because the Dark Tower contains a dedicated forge room for it.

Its integration belongs inside Tower restoration rather than in a separate Hot Iron questline.

# Conditional small arc

## Pet Cemetery / pet resurrection system

Assignment: SMALL ARC, conditionally triggered

Decision: the system should be introduced through a small authored arc only if a pet dies.

The quest chain follows the path toward resurrecting the dead pet and serves as an in-world introduction/tutorial to the resurrection system.

This is not a permanently visible generic questline and should not trigger without the relevant pet-death condition.

The implementation must remain consistent with REIGN's broader rule that ordinary sapient resurrection is exceptional and difficult. Pet resurrection is a specific supported gameplay system, not proof that all deaths are routinely reversible.

# Interview rule established by these decisions

A mod does not deserve a dedicated questline merely because it contains many mobs, advancements, structures, equipment items, or even one or more bosses.

Dedicated questlines should be reserved for content that is important enough to the intended play experience and benefits from an authored narrative/progression arc. A mechanical climax can strengthen the case, but it is not sufficient by itself.

Popup NPC acknowledgement is the preferred lightweight integration for substantial content that should feel recognized by OVERLORD REIGN without multiplying the number of full questlines.
