# OVERLORD REIGN Quest Authority and Intentional Discretion

Status: AUTHORITATIVE IMPLEMENTATION GOVERNANCE

Date: 2026-09-13

Purpose: eliminate interpretive ambiguity for Overlord Quests while preserving deliberate authorial freedom where exact quest construction belongs to the Quest Maker or depends on final implemented mechanics.

This file does not invent new lore or quest content. It defines how the existing quest authorities are to be read together.

## 1. Authority order

For quest design and implementation, use the following order when two statements appear to conflict:

1. `reference/39_REIGN_QUEST_AUTHORITY_AND_INTENTIONAL_DISCRETION.md` for authority order and interpretation rules;
2. `reference/44_REIGN_LESTAT_CONTINUITY_AUTHORITY_FINAL.md` for final REIGN-native Lestat continuity when NightWalker / Nycto or Lestat history is involved;
3. `reference/38_REIGN_MINION_TYPE_UNLOCK_ANCHORS.md` for Brown / Red / Green / Blue unlock ownership;
4. `reference/37_REIGN_PERSONAL_MOD_SIDEQUEST_DECISIONS.md` for Overlord Depths / Fathoms and Overlord NightWalker / Nycto sidequest assignment and integration direction;
5. `reference/36_REIGN_MOD_QUESTLINE_ASSIGNMENTS_FINAL.md` for reviewed-mod questline, absorption, popup, systemic, and no-integration assignments;
6. `reference/34_REIGN_TOWER_RESTORATION_DECISIONS.md` for Dark Tower room ownership and the room-restoration versus system-mastery boundary;
7. `reference/32_REIGN_QUESTLINE_COVERAGE_LEDGER.md` for minimum coverage and completion review;
8. older interview mirrors and research files only as supporting history/source context.

A later explicit decision overrides older provisional wording without requiring the older file to be interpreted creatively.

`reference/33_REIGN_MOD_QUESTLINE_ASSIGNMENTS.md` and `reference/35_REIGN_MOD_ASSIGNMENT_CONTINUATION.md` are historical interview mirrors. They do not override `36` through `39`.

`reference/41_REIGN_LESTAT_CONTINUITY_DECISIONS.md`, `reference/42_REIGN_LESTAT_CONTINUITY_INTERVIEW_CONTINUATION.md`, and `reference/43_REIGN_LESTAT_CONTINUITY_FINAL_INTERVIEW_BLOCK.md` are Lestat continuity interview-history records. `reference/44_REIGN_LESTAT_CONTINUITY_AUTHORITY_FINAL.md` consolidates them and is the implementation authority.

## 2. No remaining quest-assignment ambiguity

The following categories are considered structurally decided:

- which reviewed mods receive dedicated questlines;
- which reviewed mods receive compact or conditional arcs;
- which content is absorbed into civilization, magic, End/Wasteland, Tower, or other established questlines;
- which content receives sparse popup acknowledgement only;
- which content remains systemic, ambient, decorative, infrastructural, or has no quest-facing treatment;
- Dark Tower restoration ownership;
- Storage Room and Armory inclusion in Tower Restoration;
- the selected magic facilities that receive Tower-room restoration treatment;
- the separate status of Quaver's band questline;
- the conditional Pet Cemetery resurrection arc;
- the conditional Overlord Depths dedicated sidequest if the backport is implemented;
- the conditional Overlord NightWalker Lestat-led vampire-transition sidequest if the backport is implemented and the Overlord becomes a vampire;
- the division of responsibility for Brown / Red / Green / Blue Minion unlocks.

The Quest Maker must not reopen these assignment questions merely because another implementation would also be technically possible.

## 3. Minion unlocks: deliberate quest-author ownership

Brown, Red, Green, and Blue Minions exist in-universe.

The Minion implementation deliberately exposes quest-facing anchors/hooks rather than owning the narrative unlock progression.

Therefore:

- do not wait for a native Minion-mod unlock questline;
- do not add a second independent unlock progression to the Minion mod;
- Overlord Quests owns the authored unlock path;
- the Quest Maker may decide the exact order, prerequisites, quest framing, recovery events, Hive associations, Tower associations, and dialogue using the provided anchors;
- those details may remain spoiler-heavy in the Overlord Quests implementation repository.

This is intentional design freedom, not an unresolved implementation gap.

`reference/38_REIGN_MINION_TYPE_UNLOCK_ANCHORS.md` is authoritative for this boundary and supersedes older wording that implies otherwise.

## 4. Overlord Depths / Fathoms

If Overlord Depths is implemented, it receives a dedicated sidequest arc.

The Historian profession is the preferred principal NPC if the final NPC and quest mechanics support that role.

A Dredge-like discovery/investigation escalation is an approved direction, but the Quest Maker must construct the actual story around the final implemented Fathoms mechanics rather than copying Dredge or inventing unsupported mechanics.

The exact objectives, escalation, structure use, boss use, item requirements, and climax are intentionally implementation-dependent.

That implementation dependence is not permission to omit the sidequest.

## 5. Overlord NightWalker / Nycto

If Overlord NightWalker is implemented, the vampire-transition sidequest is mandatory when the Overlord becomes a vampire through the implemented system.

Lestat joins the Dark Tower and acts as the principal guide through the transition.

For Lestat authoring, responsibilities are split deliberately:

- `reference/44_REIGN_LESTAT_CONTINUITY_AUTHORITY_FINAL.md` controls what actually happened in Lestat's REIGN-native history, which retained IWTV figures exist, how the histories are adapted, and what continuity facts are permitted;
- `reference/40_REIGN_LESTAT_CHARACTER_AND_WRITING_RULES.md` controls voice, psychology, dialogue mechanics, source-confidence discipline, and character-writing behavior;
- the supplied IWTV transcript corpus is source evidence for characterization and must not be mistaken for a second in-universe timeline;
- final NightWalker / Nycto implementation controls operative vampire mechanics.

Lestat must not be written as a transported crossover character and must not turn his adopted background into an Easter-egg inventory.

The exact vampire-state hooks, powers, weaknesses, progression steps, objectives, and climax are intentionally dependent on the final NightWalker implementation.

That implementation dependence is not permission to omit the sidequest once its trigger conditions exist.

## 6. Gnarl and Lestat

The established direction is that Gnarl does not oppose vampirism on moral grounds. His evaluation is pragmatic and centered on the Overlord, Minions, Netherworld, and Tower continuity.

The intended relationship with Lestat is adversarial, witty, and functional rather than irrationally hostile. Lestat is useful because he possesses expertise Gnarl lacks about vampirism, which intrudes on Gnarl's traditional advisory domain.

Both ultimately want the Overlord to survive and become stronger, but they understand and advise the transformation from incompatible positions.

Detailed dialogue remains subject to the source-grounded Gnarl writing rules and the transcript-grounded Lestat characterization corpus.

This file treats the relationship direction as an approved design constraint. It does not require every exchange to be argumentative or comedic.

## 7. Tower Restoration versus deeper progression

Tower Restoration owns the initial installation, furnishing, activation, or operational recovery of Tower facilities.

Deeper mastery of the systems housed in those rooms remains with their own progression or questlines.

Current explicit Tower restoration functions include:

- Throne room;
- Forge;
- Minion infrastructure;
- Gates;
- Treasury;
- Storage Room;
- Armory;
- selected magical facilities for Alchemy, Theurgy, Gluttony, spell study/making, and Eidolon.

Quaver's band remains a separate optional Tower-personnel questline and is not required for formal Tower Restoration completion.

Do not create standalone mod questlines merely to restore a Tower room, and do not absorb whole mod progressions into Tower Restoration merely because their machinery is housed in the Tower.

## 8. Intentional Quest Maker discretion

The following are intentionally left to the Quest Maker unless a future explicit decision narrows them:

- exact quest names;
- exact dialogue wording;
- exact objective counts;
- exact branch ordering where existing prerequisites do not force an order;
- exact Brown / Red / Green / Blue unlock narratives and ordering;
- exact use of Minion anchors/hooks;
- exact Fathoms sidequest escalation and climax within final mechanics;
- exact NightWalker transition steps and climax within final mechanics;
- exact Quaver band membership, instruments, and detailed conclusion;
- exact boss selection from content assigned as optional monster-hunting or standalone episodes;
- exact reward quantities unless an established system or balance constraint requires a value;
- implementation details for optional objectives if Questlog support imposes technical limitations;
- spoiler-heavy campaign writing and branch details that do not contradict established canon or player-experience constraints;
- low-impact Lestat continuity details explicitly left UNKNOWN or delegated by `44`, such as exact dates, minor geography, or present locations of distant background characters where no quest depends on them.

These are authoring responsibilities, not unanswered design questions.

The Quest Maker should not ask the Overlord for approval of ordinary decisions in this category.

## 9. When the Quest Maker must ask the Overlord

Ask only when implementation exposes a genuinely unresolved choice that:

- conflicts with established OVERLORD REIGN canon;
- would create a new major lore fact;
- would materially change the player's campaign structure or ending access;
- would alter an established civilization outcome framework;
- would require promoting an existing PROPOSAL to CANON where approval has not already been given;
- cannot be safely inferred from the source games, installed mod mechanics, or these authority files;
- or is technically impossible without changing an established design commitment.

Do not ask merely because several ordinary quest-writing options are available.

## 10. Technical truth outranks speculative design

A quest must not demand a mechanic that the final installed mod or integration hook cannot actually support.

When a planned quest concept meets a technical limitation:

1. preserve the established narrative/design intent where possible;
2. choose the least invasive technically valid implementation;
3. use existing events, advancements, items, structures, APIs, markers, or narrow compatibility hooks before modifying another mod solely for quest tracking;
4. ask the Overlord only if the limitation would materially change the established experience or lore.

## 11. Completion test

Overlord Quests is not content-complete until it has deliberately reconciled implementation against `32`, `34`, `36`, `37`, `38`, `39`, and, for NightWalker/Lestat content, `40` plus `44`.

Completion does not mean every installed mod appears in the quest log.

Completion means:

- every required dedicated arc exists;
- every required conditional arc is wired to its trigger;
- every absorbed system appears only where appropriate;
- popup-only content remains restrained;
- no-integration content remains un-questified;
- Tower-owned restoration work remains in Tower Restoration;
- Brown / Red / Green / Blue unlock anchors receive an authored quest treatment;
- personal backport sidequests are included when their backports and trigger conditions exist;
- NightWalker Lestat dialogue and continuity respect both the final REIGN-native continuity authority and the transcript-grounded writing authority;
- technically impossible requirements are explicitly deferred or adapted rather than silently omitted.

## 12. Interpretation rule

`UNKNOWN` means the project genuinely does not yet know the answer.

`IMPLEMENTATION-DEPENDENT` means the desired design outcome is decided but exact mechanics must follow the implemented mod/API/hook reality.

`QUEST-MAKER DISCRETION` means the Overlord has deliberately delegated ordinary authored construction to the Quest Maker within established constraints.

These three statuses must not be conflated.

A future author must not turn implementation-dependent or delegated details back into broad design questions unless a real conflict or impossibility is discovered.
