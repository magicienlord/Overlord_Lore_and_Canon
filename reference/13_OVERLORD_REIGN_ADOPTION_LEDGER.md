# OVERLORD REIGN Franchise Adoption Ledger

Status: PROJECT RECONCILIATION LEDGER

Date: 2026-09-11

Authority: this file records the relationship between verified franchise evidence and decisions already present in the OVERLORD REIGN canonical project files. It does not create new REIGN canon by itself.

## Adoption vocabulary

- `ADOPTED`: preserved substantially as source-established history, rule, identity, or world element.
- `ADAPTED`: source concept preserved but deliberately reinterpreted, extended, renamed, or integrated for REIGN/Minecraft.
- `REJECTED`: explicitly not binding in REIGN.
- `RESERVED`: valid franchise material deliberately available for selective future use, but not presently established in REIGN.
- `UNDECIDED`: a decision is still required.

Scope matters. `ADOPTED: HISTORICAL` does not imply the same institution or location still exists in the present REIGN era.

## 1. Continuity and succession

### R-001: Overlord I -> Raising Hell -> Overlord II historical continuity

Franchise evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`.

REIGN status: `ADOPTED: HISTORICAL`.

Basis: `04_HISTORY.md` establishes Overlord II as REIGN's immediate major narrative predecessor and inherits the earlier continuity leading into it unless specifically revised.

Consequence: the pre-Overlord-I ruler, Overlord I campaign, Raising Hell loss in the Infernal Abyss, Great Cataclysm, and Overlord II era may be treated as ancient history subject to branch-sensitive exceptions.

REIGN status: `UNDECIDED`.

Basis: the present Overlord's ancestry and method of succession remain UNKNOWN in `04_HISTORY.md`.

### R-004: Overlord II player's father

Franchise evidence: `UNKNOWN` in current primary localization.

REIGN status: `UNDECIDED / DO NOT ASSUME`.

Do not use Marius, the Overlord I ruler, or another named figure as the father without stronger source evidence or an explicit REIGN decision.

### R-005: Overlord II Domination versus Destruction ending

Franchise evidence: `BRANCH-DEPENDENT`.
