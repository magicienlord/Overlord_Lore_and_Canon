# OVERLORD REIGN Franchise Adoption Ledger

Status: PROJECT RECONCILIATION LEDGER

Date: 2026-09-11

Authority: this file records the relationship between verified franchise evidence and decisions already present in the OVERLORD REIGN canonical project files. It does not create new REIGN canon by itself.

## Adoption vocabulary

- `ADOPTED`: preserved substantially as source-established history, rule, identity, or world element.
- `ADAPTED`: source concept preserved but deliberately reinterpreted, extended, renamed, or integrated for REIGN/Minecraft.
- `REJECTED`: explicitly not binding in REIGN.
- `RESERVED`: valid franchise material deliberately available for selective future use, but not presently established in REIGN.
- `UNDECIDED`: a decision is still required.

Scope matters. `ADOPTED: HISTORICAL` does not imply the same institution or location still exists in the present REIGN era.

## 1. Continuity and succession

### R-001: Overlord I -> Raising Hell -> Overlord II historical continuity

Franchise evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`.

REIGN status: `ADOPTED: HISTORICAL`.

Basis: `04_HISTORY.md` establishes Overlord II as REIGN's immediate major narrative predecessor and inherits the earlier continuity leading into it unless specifically revised.

Consequence: the pre-Overlord-I ruler, Overlord I campaign, Raising Hell loss in the Infernal Abyss, Great Cataclysm, and Overlord II era may be treated as ancient history subject to branch-sensitive exceptions.

### R-002: Multiple Overlords can succeed one another

Franchise evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`.

REIGN status: `ADOPTED AS FRANCHISE HISTORY`.

Multiple Overlords exist historically across the inherited franchise chronology. REIGN no longer inserts additional rulers between the Overlord II ruler and the current player.

### R-003: One universal hereditary succession law

Franchise evidence: `NOT ESTABLISHED`; hereditary potential exists but succession also involves preparation/recognition.

REIGN status: `REJECTED AS A REQUIREMENT / CURRENT SUCCESSION ADAPTED`.

The current Overlord is anointed directly by the Netherworld. No bloodline proof is required. A hereditary connection may exist but is intentionally UNKNOWN and need never be answered.

### R-004: Overlord II player's father

Franchise evidence: `UNKNOWN` in current primary localization.

REIGN status: `UNDECIDED / DO NOT ASSUME`.

Do not use Marius, the Overlord I ruler, or another named figure as the father without stronger source evidence or an explicit REIGN decision.

### R-005: Overlord II Domination versus Destruction ending

Franchise evidence: `BRANCH-DEPENDENT`.

REIGN status: `ADOPTED BRANCH SELECTION: DOMINATION`.

The Overlord II ruler follows a predominantly Domination-oriented historical reign. Where the source presents a direct Domination versus Destruction resolution and no later REIGN exception is established, Domination is the default historical outcome.

### R-006: Later post-Overlord-II sequel storylines

Franchise relationship: later franchise continuation exists outside the inherited branch.

REIGN status: `REJECTED AS BINDING STORY CONTINUITY`.

Basis: REIGN branches directly from Overlord II. Later sequel story outcomes do not determine REIGN's history.

Individual creatures, visual ideas, artifacts, or concepts from later material remain `RESERVED` for selective adoption.

Franchise evidence: not established by supplied pre-REIGN source.

REIGN status: `REJECTED / REMOVED CANON ERROR`.

Earlier REIGN drafts stated that a couple of intervening Overlords existed. That statement was an authoring error and has been superseded.

There are no intervening Overlords between the Overlord II ruler and the current REIGN Overlord.

### R-009: The Silence

Franchise evidence: not a franchise event.

REIGN status: `ADAPTED / REIGN-ORIGINAL CANON`.

The Silence begins directly after the fall or disappearance of the Overlord II ruler and continues until the current Overlord is anointed several centuries later.

The Overlord II ruler's Domination-oriented legacy leaves many civilizations alive, productive, and organized. Their later stability and complacency help explain why Evil fails to regain an Overlord-scale foothold for centuries and why the present world diverges so strongly from the old one.

The exact supernatural reason the Netherworld waits centuries before choosing another Master remains UNKNOWN.

## 4. Minions

REIGN status: `ADOPTED`.
