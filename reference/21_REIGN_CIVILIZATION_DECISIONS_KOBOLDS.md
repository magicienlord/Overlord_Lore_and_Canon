# OVERLORD REIGN Kobold Civilization Decisions

Status: PROJECT DECISION MIRROR

Date: 2026-09-11

Authority: mirrors explicit OVERLORD REIGN decisions. Numbered project canon files remain authoritative.

## Quest anchor and political scope

Status: CANON DESIGN / PLANNED IMPLEMENTATION

The Kobold civilization main quest is anchored to one designated Kobold Den.

That Den is one independent underground Kobold community rather than the capital of every Kobold in the world.

The civilization main quest begins through one protected, explicitly marked Kobold Captain in the chosen Den. Ordinary Kobold Captains, Dens, and Kobolds elsewhere do not become civilization quest starters merely through player interaction.

## Baseline behavior

Status: CANON DESIGN

Kobolds retain their mod-native behavior until the player reaches the designated Den and engages the authored quest starter.

The existence of the current Overlord does not globally alter all Kobold allegiance or AI.

## Pirate Kobolds

Status: CANON DESIGN

Pirate Kobolds are a recognizable Kobold subculture.

They remain politically independent from the main Den unless an authored quest explicitly connects them to that community or its questline.

## Subjugation

Status: CANON DESIGN / PLANNED IMPLEMENTATION

Subjugating the canonical Den affects that Den rather than every Kobold in the world.

The low-overhead benefit set is:

- safe Den access;
- Engineer services/rewards;
- Enchanter services/rewards;
- Captain and Warrior quest access;
- tribute;
- access to native Kobold trades, resources, loot, and underground services where supported by the installed mod.

No separate Kobold army, automated mining system, or new military simulation should be invented unless an existing mechanic provides it essentially for free.

## Destruction

Status: CANON DESIGN

Destroying the canonical Den is a local anchor outcome.

Its providers, peaceful services, and local quest pools may disappear or change permanently, while Kobolds and pirate groups elsewhere remain extant.

## Dialogue and intelligence

Status: CANON PRESENTATION RULE

Kobolds are generally relatively low-intelligence, and their dialogue should reflect that rather than presenting them as uniformly articulate scholars.

However, intelligence and speech quality vary by role.

- Captains are somewhat more coherent and strategically capable than ordinary Kobolds.
- Engineers and Enchanters are noticeably sharper within their own technical or magical domains, though still recognizably Kobold in reasoning and speech.
- Pirates are rougher, opportunistic, and practical.
- Rascals are more impulsive and simple-minded.
- ordinary Kobolds remain comparatively dim.

Functional specialization does not imply uniformly high intelligence. Kobold society can support Captains, Engineers, Enchanters, Warriors, Pirates, children, and other roles while still retaining a generally foolish or limited conversational character.
