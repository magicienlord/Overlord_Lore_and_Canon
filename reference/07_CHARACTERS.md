## Gnarl

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`.

Role:

- Minion Master;
- adviser and instructor to successive Overlords;
- institutional memory of Minion/Tower history;
- quest narrator, tactical commentator, and Tower/Netherworld administrator.

The supplied Overlord I and Raising Hell workbooks explicitly identify `GNARL` as actor for 911 English rows combined. A separate direct-map reconstruction now attributes 173 unique decoded Overlord II lines to Gnarl without relying on voice or stylistic guessing.

Detailed speech analysis is maintained in `09_GNARL_DIALOGUE_AND_VOICE.md`.

## The pre-Overlord-I ruler / possessed Wizard

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`; personal name `UNKNOWN` in the reviewed localization.

At the start of Overlord I he is presented as the player's predecessor, apparently killed by the hero group.

The final-battle source reveals that he survived by purging the Wizard's soul and occupying/merging with the Wizard's mortal body.

He:

- was defeated by the hero group;
- survived through the Wizard's body;
- corrupted the surviving heroes;
- arranged for the abandoned future Overlord I player to be recovered and healed by Minions;
- deliberately prepared that player as his successor;
- later attempted to eliminate the successor when he ceased to be useful;
- explicitly identifies Rose and Velvet as his daughters.

The original Wizard existed as a distinct person, but the current primary corpus provides little independent characterization of him before possession. Do not assign the possessed ruler's later personality to the original Wizard.

## Overlord I / Raising Hell player

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`.

Before becoming Overlord, he belonged to the hero group that defeated the preceding ruler. His companions abandoned him for dead; Minions recovered and healed him as the predecessor's intended successor.

He rebuilds the Tower, confronts his corrupted former companions and predecessor, remains Master through Raising Hell, defeats the Forgotten God, and is ultimately trapped/lost in the Infernal Abyss.

Overlord II directly continues from the disappearance of this prior Master.

## Rose

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`.

Overlord I establishes Rose as one of the two daughters of the pre-Overlord-I ruler and as a possible Mistress of the new Overlord.

Her source characterization is more restrained and order-oriented than Velvet's.

Overlord II establishes that:

- Rose survived into the later period;
- she is known as Mrs Marius;
- she is the mother of the Overlord II player;
- she deliberately kept him away from his Minions and Overlord birthright;
- she initially supported the Empire as a possible source of order but later regarded Solarius as corrupted by power.

The source does not explicitly identify the Overlord II player's father.

## Velvet

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`.

Overlord I establishes Velvet as Rose's sister and another daughter of the pre-Overlord-I ruler.

She is a possible Mistress and is characterized as favoring more overt displays of Evil, luxury, spectacle, bloodshed, and conspicuous tyranny than Rose.

The reviewed Overlord II source does not establish an equivalent later historical role for Velvet.

## Minion Jester

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`.

A distinct Minion court figure present in Overlord I.

Functions include:

- provoking/testing the new Overlord during the opening tutorial;
- comic commentary;
- ceremonial praise and insults;
- dynamically awarding/reacting with Tower titles tied to player accomplishments and choices.

The title system is important quest-design precedent because it provides diegetic long-term recognition outside the formal objective UI.

## The corrupted hero group

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`.

The final-battle source confirms that the major hero figures confronted during Overlord I were the player-character's former companions from the assault on the preceding Overlord.

The old Overlord states that he corrupted them one by one after their victory.

The quest-relevant group includes Melvin, Oberon, Sir William, Goldo, Jewel, and Kahn. Their expanded dossiers are in `07B_SECONDARY_CHARACTER_DOSSIERS.md`.

## Forgotten God

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`.

Principal supernatural antagonist of Raising Hell.

He is defined by being forgotten and by a desire to restore fear/remembrance of his name through suffering and death in the Abysses.

`EXP_D5_Abyss.8ld` states that he was formerly the spouse of the Mother Goddess and was cursed by her after infidelity.

After defeat he traps/curses the Overlord with the Infernal Abyss, creating the direct continuity bridge to the missing Master referenced in Overlord II.

## Overlord II player

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`.

Found as a child/foundling in Nordberg and stigmatized as a witch-boy.

The Minions recognize potential in him and the Netherworld later accepts him as its true Master.

Rose is explicitly his mother, giving him maternal descent from the earlier Overlord line through Rose.

His father remains `UNKNOWN` in the reviewed primary localization.

His reign can culminate in Destruction, Domination, or a mixed pattern depending on accumulated choices.

## Kelda

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`.

A Nordberg woman with a long-standing connection to the Overlord II player. She is available as a Mistress/First Mistress and is directly tied to Nordberg's local social context.

As Mistress she has a gameplay association with Brown Minion wolf mounts.

No additional REIGN-era legacy is assumed.

## Queen Fay

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`; later state `BRANCH-DEPENDENT`.

A major Light-magic/Sanctuary figure and ruler associated with opposition to the Empire's persecution of magical beings.

She can enter materially different later states, including Dark Fay and Ghost Fay outcomes, depending on player action.

Later historical references must not silently choose one branch unless REIGN explicitly adopts it.

## Juno

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`.

An Empire-associated woman who becomes available as a Mistress in Overlord II. Ambient and Netherworld material emphasizes beauty, status, vanity, social ambition, and adjustment to the Overlord's court.

As Mistress she has a gameplay association with mobility support for Green Minions.

No later canonical legacy is inferred.

## Marius

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`.

An important Empire official and Emperor Solarius's official spokesperson. Rose is identified as his wife in the later period. Primary source also identifies Borius as his brother and associates Marius with the Sentinels.

The reviewed source does not identify him as the Overlord II player's father.

Do not infer paternity from marriage alone.

## Florian Greenheart

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT` core facts; final-state mechanism partly `UNKNOWN`.

Florian identifies himself with the Soldiers of the Sanctuary, protectors of Sanctuaries and magical/natural creatures.

Primary source establishes that:

- he was an Elf who lacked the magical talent expected of Elves;
- he was mocked for this deficiency;
- he attempted to take/use the previous Overlord's Tower Heart to unlock magical potential;
- that attempt destabilized/exploded the Heart and caused the Great Cataclysm;
- he later serves Queen Fay and the Sanctuary cause.

The final Solarius sequence uses Florian in a host-like/instrumental magical relationship. The exact mechanism remains insufficiently explicit for a stronger metaphysical claim.

## Emperor Solarius

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`.

Solarius rules the Glorious Empire and builds his power in the post-Cataclysm climate of fear and hostility toward magic.

The Empire persecutes and processes magical beings while the final campaign material establishes elite gathering/refining/use of magical energy, culminating in Solarius's attempted ascension/transformation.

The institutional structure beneath him is expanded in `07B_SECONDARY_CHARACTER_DOSSIERS.md`.
