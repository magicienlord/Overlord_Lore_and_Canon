# OVERLORD Franchise Character Reference

Status: PRIMARY-SOURCE SYNTHESIS, INITIAL QUEST-RELEVANT SET

Date: 2026-09-11

Authority: franchise reference only. This is not yet an exhaustive cast list. It prioritizes characters whose history, relationships, or voice materially affect future OVERLORD REIGN quest writing.

## Gnarl

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`

Role:

- Minion Master;
- adviser and instructor to successive Overlords;
- institutional memory of Minion/Tower history;
- quest narrator, tactical commentator, and Tower/Netherworld administrator.

The supplied Overlord I and Raising Hell workbooks explicitly identify `GNARL` as actor for 911 English rows combined.

Gnarl survives changes of Overlord and retains knowledge of prior Masters. He is therefore one of the strongest continuity anchors across the franchise.

Detailed speech analysis is maintained separately in `09_GNARL_DIALOGUE_AND_VOICE.md`.

## The pre-Overlord-I ruler / possessed Wizard

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`; personal name `UNKNOWN` in the reviewed localization.

At the start of Overlord I he is presented as the player's predecessor, apparently killed by heroes.

The final-battle source reveals that he survived by purging the Wizard's soul and occupying/merging with the Wizard's mortal body.

He:

- was defeated by the hero group;
- survived through the Wizard's body;
- corrupted the surviving heroes;
- arranged for the abandoned future Overlord I player to be recovered and healed by Minions;
- deliberately prepared that player as his successor;
- later attempted to eliminate the successor when he ceased to be useful;
- explicitly identifies Rose and Velvet as his daughters.

This character is central to understanding Overlord succession and the corruption of the original heroes.

## Overlord I / Raising Hell player

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`

Before becoming Overlord, he belonged to the hero group that defeated the preceding ruler.

He was left for dead by his companions, recovered by Minions, healed, and prepared as successor.

He rebuilds the Tower, confronts the corrupted heroes and his predecessor, remains Master through Raising Hell, defeats the Forgotten God, and is ultimately trapped/lost in the Infernal Abyss.

Overlord II refers back to this lost predecessor.

## Rose

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`

Overlord I establishes Rose as one of the two daughters of the pre-Overlord-I ruler and as a possible Mistress of the new Overlord.

Her source characterization is more restrained and order-oriented than Velvet's. The private-quarters and choice material contrasts the sisters' attitudes toward the Overlord's conduct.

Overlord II later establishes that:

- Rose survived into the later period;
- she is known as Mrs Marius;
- she is the mother of the Overlord II player;
- she deliberately kept him away from his Minions and Overlord birthright;
- she initially supported the Empire as a possible source of order but came to regard Solarius as corrupted by power.

The source does not yet explicitly identify the Overlord II player's father.

## Velvet

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`

Overlord I establishes Velvet as Rose's sister and another daughter of the pre-Overlord-I ruler.

She is a possible Mistress choice and is explicitly characterized in production notes as favoring more overt displays of Evil than Rose.

Her private-quarters material emphasizes luxury, spectacle, bloodshed, and conspicuous tyranny more strongly than Rose's.

The current Overlord II localization review has not established an equivalent later historical role for Velvet.

## Minion Jester

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`

A distinct Minion court figure present in Overlord I.

Functions include:

- provoking/testing the new Overlord during the opening tutorial;
- comic commentary;
- ceremonial praise and insults;
- dynamically awarding/reacting with Tower titles tied to player accomplishments and choices.

The Jester's title system is important quest-design precedent because it gives diegetic long-term recognition to choices outside the formal objective UI.

## The corrupted hero group

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`

The final-battle source confirms that the major hero figures confronted during Overlord I were the player-character's former companions from the assault on the preceding Overlord.

The old Overlord states that he corrupted them one by one after their victory.

Known figures represented in the final source include Melvin, Oberon, Sir William, Goldo, Jewel, and Kahn.

Their individual regional histories remain to be expanded in a later character pass.

## Oberon

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`

Oberon's own dialogue describes abandoning his people/forest, being punished, losing control under invasive roots/nightmares, and ultimately asking the Overlord to end his torment.

His death dialogue recognizes that the Overlord had the strength to do what Oberon himself could not.

This is useful precedent for an antagonist who is both culpable and a victim of corruption.

## Sir William

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`

Source dialogue identifies him as a former defender of right/justice who embraces pain, fleshly pleasure, violence, and liberation from morality after the Wizard's influence.

He explicitly credits the Wizard with teaching him to seek this "freedom".

## Jewel

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`

Jewel is defined through theft, opportunism, and mercenary logic. She leads/works with raiders and is involved in stealing major objects on the Wizard's behalf.

She also has a volatile relationship with Kahn, whose obsession with her drives later events.

## Kahn

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`

A physically destructive figure driven heavily by attachment to Jewel and escalating anger. His later Raising Hell appearance shows the continuation of that fixation in the Abyss.

The source repeatedly treats his emotional dependence and rage as exploitable weaknesses.

## Forgotten God

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`

Principal supernatural antagonist of Raising Hell.

He is defined by being forgotten, by a desire to make his name feared/remembered again, and by using suffering/death in the Abysses toward that restoration.

`EXP_D5_Abyss.8ld` states that he was formerly the spouse of the Mother Goddess and was cursed by her after infidelity.

After defeat he traps/curses the Overlord with the Infernal Abyss, creating the direct bridge to the missing Master referenced in Overlord II.

## Overlord II player

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`

Found as a child/foundling in Nordberg and stigmatized locally as a witch-boy.

The Minions recognize potential in him and later the Netherworld accepts him as its true Master.

Rose is explicitly his mother, giving him maternal descent from an earlier Overlord through her lineage.

His father remains `UNKNOWN` in the reviewed localization.

His reign can culminate in Destruction, Domination, or a mixed pattern depending on accumulated choices.

## Kelda

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`

A Nordberg woman with a long-standing connection to the Overlord II player. Source material describes her as coming from Nordberg, capable of violence, and available as a Mistress/First Mistress.

As Mistress she provides a gameplay association with Brown Minion wolf mounts.

A fuller personal-history pass is still required before using her as historical exposition in REIGN.

## Queen Fay

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`

A major Light-magic / Sanctuary figure and ruler associated with opposition to the Empire's persecution of magical beings.

She can enter radically different states depending on player action, with source material distinguishing transformed Dark Fay and Ghost Fay outcomes.

Because her condition is branch-dependent, later historical references should not silently choose one version unless OVERLORD REIGN explicitly decides which branch, if any, it inherits.

## Juno

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`

An Empire-associated woman who becomes available as a Mistress in Overlord II. Ambient and Netherworld material emphasizes beauty, status, vanity, social ambition, and her adjustment to the Overlord's court.

As Mistress she has a gameplay association with mobility support for Green Minions.

Her precise political history should receive a dedicated pass before any REIGN-era legacy is established.

## Marius

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT`

An important Empire official and official spokesperson for Emperor Solarius. Rose is identified as his wife in the later period.

The current reviewed source does not identify him as the Overlord II player's father.

Do not infer paternity from marriage alone.

## Florian Greenheart

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT` core facts; final-state mechanics partly `UNRESOLVED`.

Florian introduces himself as `Florian Greenheart` and identifies himself with the Soldiers of the Sanctuary, protectors of Sanctuaries and magical/natural creatures.

Later source establishes that:

- he was an Elf who lacked the magical talent expected of Elves;
- he was mocked for this deficiency;
- he attempted to steal the previous Overlord's Tower Heart to unlock magical potential;
- that attempt destabilized/exploded the Heart and caused the Great Cataclysm;
- he later serves as Queen Fay's lieutenant.

The final Solarius sequence treats Florian as being retained/used in a host-like or instrumental state. The exact magical mechanism connecting Florian and Solarius in those scenes remains to be reconstructed before making a stronger claim.

## Emperor Solarius

Evidence: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT` core political role.

Solarius rules the Glorious Empire and builds his power in the post-Cataclysm climate of fear and hostility toward magic.

The Empire persecutes/"cleanses" magic users and magical creatures under his order.

Final-battle material establishes that Solarius gathers, refines, and redistributes magical energy through his regime while consuming some himself, culminating in an attempted divine ascension and catastrophic magical transformation.

The exact mechanics of his relationship with Florian during the final sequence remain a source-analysis task rather than a settled entry here.
