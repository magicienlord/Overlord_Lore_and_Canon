# OVERLORD REIGN Umvuthana Civilization Decisions

Status: PROJECT DECISION MIRROR

Date: 2026-09-11

Authority: mirrors explicit OVERLORD REIGN decisions. Numbered project canon files remain authoritative.

## Origin and chronology

Status: CANON / REIGN ADAPTATION

Umvuthi remains literally the creator of the Umvuthana as established by Mowzie's Mobs.

This does not require the biological substrate from which he made them to be newly created from nothing. REIGN permits the Umvuthana to have been formed by Umvuthi from a pre-existing forest-derived people while still treating Umvuthi as the creator of the present Umvuthana people.

The exact deeper ancestry of that substrate remains UNKNOWN.

Umvuthi and the first Umvuthana arose during the Silence in ecological and cultural space created by the Glorious Empire's massacre and suppression of Elven populations. Their emergence is therefore historically tied to the destruction of the Elves without making Umvuthi an ancient participant in the Overlord II era.

## Grove political structure

Status: CANON DESIGN

Umvuthana society is decentralized into Grove-polities.

Each Grove may have its own Umvuthi, who functions as the local creator-god, ruler, and theological center of the Umvuthana associated with that Grove.

The designated canonical Grove is one such polity rather than the universal capital of all Umvuthana.

Other Umvuthis and their Groves remain independent unless separately affected by authored quests.

## Quest starter and initial disposition

Status: CANON DESIGN / PLANNED IMPLEMENTATION

The civilization main quest begins through the Umvuthi of the designated canonical Grove.

Umvuthi is the protected quest-critical NPC for that anchor. His native hostile state is preserved before the player establishes a legitimate audience.

A peaceful route should reuse the native mask-recognition mechanic: approaching while wearing an Umvuthana mask makes Umvuthi non-hostile and allows the Overlord to begin the authored neutral route.

Once the first audience/peaceful-contact milestone is completed, the canonical Grove enters NEUTRAL and should no longer require the Overlord to keep wearing the mask simply to avoid renewed hostility.

Attacking Umvuthi or deliberately entering the destructive branch may break that relationship.

## Subjugation

Status: CANON DESIGN / PLANNED IMPLEMENTATION

If the canonical Umvuthi submits, the authored followers of his Grove submit with him because he is their creator-god and political center.

This affects only the canonical Grove. Wild hunting groups and other Groves ruled by other Umvuthis remain independent unless separately addressed.

The low-overhead benefit set should reuse existing Mowzie's Mobs systems where possible:

- safe Grove access;
- Umvuthi and Umvuthana trading/services;
- masks and related native rewards;
- Sun-related rewards and blessings;
- tribute;
- Umvuthana sidequests and local services.

No new autonomous Umvuthana army or production simulation is required.

## Destruction

Status: CANON DESIGN

Killing the canonical Umvuthi is the local destructive resolution and should preserve the native boss-fight path and its associated rewards.

That outcome permanently breaks the canonical Grove's neutral/subjugated quest path and may destroy or destabilize the local divine order.

Other Umvuthis and Umvuthana Groves survive elsewhere.

## Religious and tonal rule

Status: CANON PRESENTATION RULE

The Umvuthana sincerely worship their local Umvuthi as their living creator-god.

Subjugating the canonical Grove should therefore be written as the deliberate humiliation of a living deity forced to acknowledge the Overlord as the greater power while his worshippers witness it.

This is intended to preserve the black-comic triumph and cruelty characteristic of Overlord rather than reduce Umvuthi to an ordinary tribal chief.
