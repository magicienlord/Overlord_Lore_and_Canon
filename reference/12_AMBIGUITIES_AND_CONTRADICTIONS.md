# OVERLORD Franchise Ambiguities and Contradictions Ledger

Status: PRIMARY-SOURCE RECONCILIATION

Date: 2026-09-11

Authority: franchise evidence only. This file exists to stop uncertain, branch-dependent, implementation-only, or apparently contradictory material from being silently upgraded into settled history.

## Status vocabulary

- `RESOLVED-SURFACE-CONFLICT`: primary sources initially appear inconsistent, but another primary source explains the discrepancy.
- `BRANCH-DEPENDENT`: multiple outcomes are intentionally supported by player choice.
- `PRIMARY-IMPLIED`: strongly supported but not stated directly enough to call explicit.
- `OPEN-AMBIGUITY`: source supports multiple interpretations or leaves a mechanism undefined.
- `IMPLEMENTATION-VARIANT`: supplied source files represent different versions/build states rather than a lore contradiction.
- `REIGN-ADAPTATION-BOUNDARY`: OVERLORD REIGN has made a stronger or different worldbuilding decision than the literal franchise terminology.

## A-001: Was the pre-Overlord-I ruler killed by the heroes?

Status: `RESOLVED-SURFACE-CONFLICT`

Early source:

- `Throneroom_Intro.8ld` has Gnarl say the heroes killed the player's predecessor.

Later source:

- `End_Tower_Battle_Narrative.8ld` reveals that the predecessor survived by purging the Wizard's soul and becoming one with the Wizard's mortal body.

Resolution:

The opening account reflects what was believed/communicated before the final reveal. The finale supplies the more complete history. Do not record two separate predecessor deaths.

## A-002: What is the Overlord I player's relationship to his predecessor?

Status: `OPEN-AMBIGUITY`

The finale explicitly establishes that the Overlord I player was one of the heroes who defeated the predecessor, was abandoned by the other heroes, was recovered/healed by Minions, and was deliberately prepared by the predecessor as a successor.

The reviewed source does not establish a blood relationship between them.

Do not infer father/son, dynasty, reincarnation, or identical identity.

## A-003: Who is the Overlord II player's father?

Status: `OPEN-AMBIGUITY`

Explicit facts:

- Rose is his mother.
- Rose is a daughter of the pre-Overlord-I ruler.
- Gnarl calls Overlordship the player's birthright.
- Rose is later known as Mrs Marius.

The reviewed localization does not explicitly identify Marius, the Overlord I player, or any other named person as the player's father.

Paternity remains `UNKNOWN`.

## A-004: Is Overlord succession hereditary?

Status: `PRIMARY-IMPLIED / OPEN-AMBIGUITY`

The Overlord II player demonstrably has hereditary Overlord ancestry through Rose and is described in terms of birthright.

The Overlord I succession is instead explicitly engineered/prepared by the previous ruler and Minions, with no established blood relationship.

The source therefore supports hereditary potential and supernatural/Minion recognition, but not one universal succession law.

## A-005: Which Overlord I Mistress is historical?

Status: `BRANCH-DEPENDENT`

Rose and Velvet are mutually exclusive player-choice Mistress outcomes.

Both sisters and their shared lineage are source facts. Which one becomes the Overlord I player's Mistress is not universal history unless REIGN selects a branch.

## A-006: What is Queen Fay's final state?

Status: `BRANCH-DEPENDENT`

Overlord II supports materially different Queen Fay outcomes, including Dark Fay and Ghost Fay states.

Later historical use must not combine incompatible outcomes.

## A-007: Who is the Overlord II First Mistress?

Status: `BRANCH-DEPENDENT`

Kelda, Fay/Dark Fay, and Juno participate in branch-sensitive Mistress/court outcomes.

Do not treat one final court composition as universal unless explicitly adopted.

## A-008: Is the Overlord II ending Domination or Destruction?

Status: `BRANCH-DEPENDENT`

`End_Scene.8ld` contains Destruction-oriented, mixed, and Domination-oriented summary outcomes.

OVERLORD REIGN currently preserves this as `UNKNOWN`, which is source-correct.

## A-009: Are the Netherworld and the Evil/Infernal Abysses the same realm?

Status: `OPEN-AMBIGUITY`

The games use the terms differently and give them different functions:

- Netherworld: Minion refuge, seat of Overlord power, construction domain, Graveyard, Gates, Barracks, Hives/infrastructure.
- Abysses: punishment/damnation spaces, Wraiths and Evil dead, Abyss Stones, Forgotten God, Infernal Abyss.

The source establishes a distinction in terminology and function but does not define their exact cosmological relationship.

Do not collapse them into one place and do not assert they are cosmologically unrelated.

## A-010: Is Lifeforce the same thing as a soul?

Status: `OPEN-AMBIGUITY`

Lifeforce is a fungible harvestable resource used to support/summon Minions.

Individual Minion souls persist after death and can be selected for resurrection.

This strongly implies a distinction between raw Lifeforce and persistent individual identity, but the precise metaphysical conversion is not defined.

OVERLORD REIGN's soul/Lifeforce model is therefore an adaptation grounded in source behavior, not a verbatim franchise formula.

## A-011: Does everyone reincarnate in an Abyss after death?

Status: `OPEN-AMBIGUITY`

Raising Hell explicitly shows Melvin repeatedly reincarnating as part of his punishment and shows several dead antagonists persisting as spirits in individualized Abysses.

Other source material shows different post-death states, including ghosts, Wraiths, Minion souls, and Ghost Fay.

No universal reincarnation rule is established.

## A-012: Does the Mother Goddess objectively exist?

Status: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT CULTURAL AND SUPERNATURAL CLAIM`, exact metaphysics `OPEN-AMBIGUITY`.

Elves worship and invoke the Mother Goddess as a deity.

In `EXP_D5_Abyss.8ld`, Gnarl identifies the Forgotten God as a deity and says the Mother Goddess was his former spouse who cursed him.

This is stronger evidence than mere cultural belief. However, the source still does not define her present location, powers, degree of intervention, or relationship to all Light magic.

Do not infer that she is the universal source of Good/Light magic.

## A-013: What is Light magic relative to Mana and Evil energy?

Status: `OPEN-AMBIGUITY / REIGN-ADAPTATION-BOUNDARY`

The source uses several overlapping terms:

- Mana as a spellcasting resource;
- ambient/general magical energy;
- Light magic as good-aligned magical expression;
- Evil energy/dark energy associated with Evil creatures and the Overlord.

It does not provide a formal equation among these categories.

REIGN's `Good Mana / Evil Mana` dual-polarity system is a deliberate formalization, not literal source terminology.

## A-014: Are spell categories universal schools or institutions?

Status: `REIGN-ADAPTATION-BOUNDARY`

The games use functional spell types and Spell Stones. They do not establish a worldwide academy/school taxonomy equivalent to the installed Minecraft spell-school system.

REIGN correctly treats its schools as expression categories rather than automatically as institutions.

## A-015: What is the exact mechanism of the Great Cataclysm?

Status: `PRIMARY-EXPLICIT` cause, `OPEN-AMBIGUITY` full physics.

Explicit chain:

- Florian attempts to take/use the predecessor's Tower Heart to unlock magical potential.
- the Heart becomes unstable and explodes.
- the explosion causes the Great Cataclysm.
- released/pent-up magical energy is linked to the magical plague.

The source does not provide a complete physical or dimensional model of the explosion.

REIGN's additional End/dimensional-Wasteland consequence is an adaptation.

## A-016: Did Raising Hell occur only after every base-game event?

Status: `OPEN-CHRONOLOGY / IMPLEMENTATION-BOUNDARY`

Expansion map triggers and availability depend on base-campaign progression states. Some Abyss content is integrated into the campaign rather than represented as a single post-credits block.

The final Infernal Abyss sequence, however, provides the direct continuity bridge into the missing Master later described by Overlord II.

Do not convert unlock conditions into exact in-universe dates without narrative evidence.

## A-017: How exactly are Florian and Solarius related in the final battle?

Status: `OPEN-AMBIGUITY`

The source clearly establishes Florian's history, his role with the Sanctuaries, his Cataclysm responsibility, and Solarius' magical accumulation/ascension program.

The final sequence uses Florian in a host-like or instrumental state, but the exact magical mechanism has not yet been reconstructed strongly enough for a more precise metaphysical claim.

## A-018: How much of Goldo's behavior describes Dwarven culture?

Status: `OPEN-INTERPRETIVE BOUNDARY`

The Dwarven state is objectively associated with mining, gold, fortification, brewing, war, and Elven enslavement under Goldo.

Goldo's extreme personal obsession with gold is explicitly his characterization. It must not automatically be treated as the psychology of every Dwarf.

The same rule applies to hostile jokes or Gnarl's stereotypes about other peoples.

## A-019: Are internal quest IDs canonical names?

Status: `PRIMARY-IMPLEMENTATION ONLY`

Identifiers such as `D1_WORLDSTONE`, `LM1C_OLCHALL_05`, and other script/system IDs are valid implementation evidence for structure and state.

They are not automatically names that inhabitants use in-universe.

## A-020: Does the source number the Overlords using the project's ordinal terminology?

Status: `OPEN / NOT EXPLICIT IN CURRENT LOCALIZATION`

The decoded corpus refers to multiple Masters and even the `very First Overlord`, but the current English localization search does not itself establish a systematic First/Second/Third/Fourth ordinal naming scheme for the player rulers.

OVERLORD REIGN may use ordinal shorthand for project/history organization, but it should not cite the decoded localization as proof of every ordinal label unless another primary source explicitly supplies it.

## A-021: Source-file version differences

No demonstrated lore contradiction follows from these differences. Preserve provenance and consult the alternate Tower map only when a version-sensitive implementation question arises.

## A-022: Exact chronology and dates

Status: `OPEN-AMBIGUITY`

The corpus establishes event order around major events but does not supply a reliable absolute calendar or exact durations for many transitions, including:

- Raising Hell to the Minion Exodus;
- Exodus to the Great Cataclysm;
- Cataclysm to the magical plague and rise of the Empire;
- the full interval before Overlord II.

REIGN's several-century post-Overlord-II gap is its own canon decision.

## A-023: What survives into OVERLORD REIGN?

Status: `REIGN-ADOPTION QUESTION`

A primary-source fact can be historically true without its institution, population, location, religion, artifact, or political state surviving unchanged for several additional centuries.

Present-day survival is therefore never inferred solely from old franchise existence. It must be recorded in `13_OVERLORD_REIGN_ADOPTION_LEDGER.md` or a canonical REIGN file.
