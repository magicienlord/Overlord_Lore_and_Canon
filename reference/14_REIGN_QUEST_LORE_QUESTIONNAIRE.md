# OVERLORD REIGN Quest-Lore Decision Questionnaire

Status: INTERVIEW BACKLOG, NOT CANON

Date: 2026-09-11

Purpose: identify only the unresolved OVERLORD REIGN decisions that materially affect future main quests, sidequests, NPC dialogue, disposition outcomes, and persistent world-state writing.

Answers become CANON or PLANNED only when the Overlord explicitly decides them and the corresponding project files are updated.

## Priority classes

### QUEST-CRITICAL

A decision needed before the relevant quest arc can be authored safely.

### WORLD-COHERENCE

A decision needed for consistent dialogue/world-state writing but not necessarily before the first quest prototype.

### OPTIONAL-LORE

Useful depth that can remain UNKNOWN until a quest specifically needs it.

---

# I. Opening state and the current Overlord

## Q-001: How does the current Overlord enter the story?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL`

Already CANON:

- the current player is a new Overlord after the Silence;
- he is not the Overlord II ruler;
- the new Dark Tower rose one day before the Silence ended;
- precise origin, ancestry, previous life, and succession method are UNKNOWN.

Decision needed:

What does the player actually experience at the beginning?

Possibilities are not preselected. The answer must establish whether he awakens, arrives, is summoned, is discovered, returns from somewhere, emerges with the Tower, or another explicitly chosen event occurs.

Quest impact:

This determines the first Gnarl scene, tutorial framing, how much the player knows, and why the first main objective exists.

## Q-002: What does the current Overlord remember at the start?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL`

Decision needed:

- does he know who/what he is;
- does he remember a previous life;
- does he know Gnarl or Minions;
- does he know old Overlord history;
- is his origin deliberately mysterious even to himself?

Quest impact:

Determines whether Gnarl explains basic world/history facts to the player or speaks as though they share prior knowledge.

## Q-003: How is the current Overlord recognized as legitimate?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL`

Franchise precedent:

- Minions can recognize/search for a Master;
- the Netherworld can accept a true Master;
- succession may include hereditary potential or deliberate preparation.

REIGN decision needed:

What specific recognition event applies to the current Overlord, if any?

Do not assume bloodline, Netherworld selection, Minion vote, artifact test, or Tower recognition without a decision.

## Q-004: Is the current Overlord's ancestry relevant to the main story?

Priority: `WORLD-COHERENCE`, becomes `QUEST-CRITICAL` if lineage is a plot device.

Current status: ancestry UNKNOWN.

Decision needed only if quests will use it. It can otherwise remain permanently undefined.

## Q-005: Does the Overlord speak in authored dialogue?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL`

Decision needed:

- fully silent player, with choices represented by actions/UI;
- short authored choice responses;
- fully voiced/written protagonist dialogue;
- another explicit model.

Quest impact:

This determines the entire dialogue architecture of Overlord_Quests.

# II. Gnarl, Minions, and the Silence

## Q-006: What happened to Gnarl during the centuries and the Silence?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL`

Franchise source establishes Gnarl as long-lived institutional memory across Overlords.

REIGN has not yet established:

- whether Gnarl personally served every intervening Overlord;
- where he was during the Silence;
- whether he remained active, dormant, trapped, hidden, or otherwise occupied;
- how much he knows about why the Silence happened.

Quest impact:

This determines what the main narrator can truthfully explain.

## Q-007: What happened to the Minion population during the Silence?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL`

Need to establish enough for opening quests to answer:

- where Minions were;
- whether all four traditional tribes/colors remain available;
- whether Hives survived;
- whether their population collapsed, hid, dispersed, or remained organized;
- why they are able or unable to serve the new Overlord immediately.

Exact population numbers are not required unless implementation needs them.

## Q-008: What is the present status of the Netherworld?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL` if the Netherworld appears in early gameplay, otherwise `WORLD-COHERENCE`.

Source precedent establishes the Netherworld as Minion refuge and seat of power.

REIGN needs to decide whether it is:

- intact;
- damaged;
- inaccessible initially;
- altered during the Silence;
- still connected to Minion infrastructure;
- relevant only as lore rather than a playable realm.

Do not conflate it with the Abysses.

## Q-009: Which named legacy Minions/personnel still exist?

Priority: `OPTIONAL-LORE` until a quest uses them.

Examples include Mortis, Quaver, Grubby, or other named franchise Minions.

No legacy character needs to survive merely because Gnarl does.

# III. The Dark Tower and the first main-quest arc

## Q-010: Why did the new Dark Tower rise?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL` only if this is part of the opening mystery; otherwise it may remain `UNKNOWN` initially.

Current CANON establishes the event but not its cause.

Decision needed:

Is the cause known to Gnarl, discoverable through the main story, or intentionally unexplained?

## Q-011: What condition is the Dark Tower in when play begins?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL`

Need to establish the narrative state of:

- throne/central command;
- Minion infrastructure;
- forge;
- portals/Gates;
- treasury;
- private/court areas;
- magical systems;
- damaged or dormant sections.

This need not determine the exact Minecraft floor plan, which belongs to architecture/implementation.

## Q-012: What is Gnarl's first strategic objective for the new Overlord?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL`

The first arc needs a concrete reason to leave or expand beyond the Tower.

The answer should identify the first strategic problem or opportunity, not merely say "conquer the world."

## Q-013: What is the main narrative pressure of the early game?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL`

Examples of categories, not proposals to adopt:

- restore the Overlord's own infrastructure;
- investigate the Silence;
- recover Minion capability;
- respond to a nearby civilization;
- recover an artifact;
- confront a supernatural disturbance;
- establish territory.

The actual answer remains open.

# IV. Historical knowledge in the present era

## Q-014: How well is ancient Overlord history remembered by ordinary people?

Priority: `WORLD-COHERENCE`

Several centuries have passed.

Need a baseline for whether Overlord I, Raising Hell, the Great Cataclysm, Overlord II, and later intervening reigns are generally:

- documented history;
- fragmented history;
- folklore;
- propaganda;
- religious story;
- specialist knowledge;
- mostly forgotten.

The answer may differ by civilization.

## Q-015: How much does Gnarl know about the intervening Overlords?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL` for historical quests, otherwise `WORLD-COHERENCE`.

Their existence is CANON but their details are UNKNOWN.

We do not need to invent every reign. We need only decide whether Gnarl can reference them as known personal history or whether some reigns are themselves obscure to him.

## Q-016: Does the Silence have a known historical cause at the start?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL` if it drives the main plot.

Possible answer types:

- Gnarl knows;
- another faction knows;
- nobody knows initially;
- several competing explanations exist;
- the cause is deliberately never important.

No cause is currently established.

## Q-017: Must the Overlord II ending ever be selected?

Priority: `WORLD-COHERENCE`, usually not immediately quest-critical.

Current status: UNKNOWN and branch-sensitive.

Decision needed:

Should REIGN preserve this ambiguity indefinitely, or do we expect later lore work to select enough of the ending to support specific historical consequences?

A full Domination/Destruction score does not need to be chosen if only one specific historical outcome matters.

# V. Legacy locations and ruins

## Q-018: Can old franchise locations survive into the present world?

Priority: `WORLD-COHERENCE`

Current project rule: no old location is automatically adopted.

Decision needed at policy level:

When quests need an ancient place, may we adopt a surviving/rebuilt/ruined Spree, Evernight, Golden Hills, Nordberg, Everlight, Wasteland site, etc., one at a time?

The exact list can remain empty until needed.

## Q-019: What forms can historical location continuity take?

Priority: `WORLD-COHERENCE`

For an adopted old site, determine whether acceptable forms include:

- surviving settlement;
- rebuilt settlement with old name;
- renamed successor settlement;
- archaeological ruin;
- buried/forgotten site;
- only historical reference with no surviving physical site.

This prevents later quests from assuming all old places either vanished or survived unchanged.

## Q-020: How should procedurally discovered Minecraft settlements become named quest locations?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL FOR IMPLEMENTATION`, not lore-critical before final world generation.

Need a rule for when a generated settlement receives:

- canonical name;
- civilization affiliation;
- NPC-provider pool;
- main-quest marker;
- persistent disposition consequences.

Exact locations cannot be chosen until the final world exists.

## Q-028: What does NEUTRAL mean?

## Q-029: What makes a civilization HOSTILE?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL`.

Determine whether hostility means universal attack-on-sight, political hostility with some neutral service NPCs, blocked quest pools, periodic encounters, or another technically feasible authored state.

## Q-030: Can a resolved civilization state later change?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL FOR LONG-TERM QUEST DESIGN`.

Need rules for reversibility:

- terminal forever;
- changeable only through main quests;
- revolts possible;
- player can destroy a previously subjugated population;
- reconciliation after hostility possible;

## Q-031: What tribute, gifts, or services follow each state?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL` when rewards are implemented.

The system already establishes that gifts/tribute are effects of disposition, not reputation points.

Exact goods must be authored per civilization and should not reuse retired Reputation! interest tables as canon.

## Q-032: What Destruction/Domination-style dilemmas fit this civilization?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL`.

The original games show that Domination may involve slavery, forced labor, subjugation, or keeping useful targets alive, while Destruction may involve killing or annihilation.

REIGN does not need to force every choice into those exact two labels. The question is what recurring authored tension best expresses Overlord rule for this civilization.

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL` for multi-faction arcs.

This affects whether quests may resolve through tribute, proxy war, intimidation, forced truce, mutual subjugation, betrayal, or selective destruction.

Priority: `WORLD-COHERENCE`, quest-critical only for religious arcs.

Inherited history includes Mother Goddess worship, the Silent God order, and the Forgotten God/Abyss history.

Need not decide all immediately. For each religion used in a quest, establish whether it is:

- active;
- transformed;
- extinct;
- remembered academically;
- mythologized;
- unknown to the current population.

## Q-036: Is Mother Goddess worship still an Elven or wider living tradition?

Priority: `OPTIONAL-LORE` until an Elf/religion quest requires it.

Historical existence is adopted. Present-day religious continuity is not yet established.

## Q-037: How publicly understood are Good Mana and Evil Mana?

Priority: `WORLD-COHERENCE`.

REIGN canon formalizes these polarities, but ordinary people need not use scholarly/system terminology.

Determine whether the terms are common language, magical scholarship, Gnarl's terminology, or primarily project-level metaphysical description.

# IX. Magic in ordinary quest life

## Q-038: How common are competent magic users?

Priority: `WORLD-COHERENCE`.

The modpack makes magic mechanically broad, but the setting still needs a social baseline for whether a village wizard, hedge mage, ritualist, necromancer, or alchemist is mundane, unusual, prestigious, feared, or civilization-specific.

## Q-039: What is the current social memory of the Great Cataclysm and magical plague?

Priority: `WORLD-COHERENCE`.

Several centuries allow different interpretations. This matters if modern people fear particular forms of magic or use the Cataclysm politically.

## Q-040: Which magical practices are socially taboo versus merely dangerous?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL` for magic-focused sidequests.

REIGN already defines metaphysical ownership of Blood, Eidolon, Theurgy, Biomancy, etc. It has not yet defined how every civilization judges those practices socially.

# X. Death, souls, and quest stakes

## Q-041: How available is resurrection outside Minion systems?

Priority: `WORLD-COHERENCE`.

Souls objectively exist, but the franchise does not make ordinary resurrection universally available.

Need a REIGN rule before quests use named NPC death as a permanent stake.

## Q-042: Can ordinary dead NPCs become ghosts or other persistent spirits?

Priority: `OPTIONAL-LORE`, quest-critical for ghost stories.

Source precedent allows several forms of post-death persistence but does not establish universality.

A quest should not assume every murdered NPC remains interactable as a ghost.

## Q-043: What limits apply to soul manipulation?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL` for Eidolon/necromancy storylines.

The magic system defines ownership categories, but quest consequences need clear limits on identity, consent/control, resurrection, binding, and irreversible death when those themes become relevant.

# XI. Main quest and sidequest narrative architecture

## Q-044: What is a Main Quest marker in-universe?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL`.

Technically it gates sidequest pools. Narratively, decide whether it represents:

- political progress;
- territorial contact;
- a Gnarl-declared campaign phase;
- completion of a major local crisis;
- another explicit story milestone.

The UI marker does not need an in-universe name, but the narrative condition does.

## Q-045: Why do NPCs give the Overlord sidequests?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL`.

This should vary by provider but needs a coherent setting rule.

Potential motives may include fear, opportunism, desperation, tribute seeking, coercion, ambition, belief that the Overlord can solve something, or an attempt to manipulate him.

Do not write providers as though the Overlord were a neutral heroic adventurer unless the local situation justifies that behavior.

## Q-046: Can sidequests alter civilization disposition directly?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL`.

Need to establish whether:

- only major/main quest branches set disposition;
- sidequests can contribute explicit markers toward a later branch;
- exceptional sidequests can change state directly;
- sidequests affect only local services/dialogue.

Avoid recreating Reputation points indirectly through hidden sidequest scoring unless explicitly desired.

## Q-047: What persists after a sidequest?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL`.

Possible authored persistence includes:

- NPC dialogue;
- provider availability;
- spawned/removed NPCs;
- structure or block changes;
- services;
- gifts;
- local hostility;
- future quest prerequisites;
- Gnarl reactions.

Set a minimum persistence standard so sidequests do not feel disconnected from the world.

## Q-048: How should quest failure work?

Priority: `QUEST-CRITICAL`.

Need a general policy for:

- retryable failure;
- permanent branch failure;
- NPC death;
- abandoning quests;
- contradictory objectives;
- failure becoming a different completed outcome.

The original games often use consequence states rather than conventional RPG fail screens, which is useful precedent.

## Q-049: How much quest lore should be optional?

Priority: `WORLD-COHERENCE`.

Determine the split among:

- mandatory Gnarl lines;
- optional NPC follow-up dialogue;
- readable/codex-like information;
- ambient dialogue;
- environmental clues.

This prevents the main quest from becoming an exposition dump while still preserving the lore corpus.

# XII. Tone and content boundaries

## Q-050: How dark should ordinary sidequests be?

Priority: `WORLD-COHERENCE`.

The franchise can move from food theft and drunkenness to slavery, plague, torture, souls, and mass killing while maintaining black comedy.

REIGN needs a tonal baseline so every sidequest is not written at maximum atrocity or, conversely, sanitized into generic helpful-adventurer work.

## Q-051: What subjects should receive reduced comedy?

Priority: `WORLD-COHERENCE`.

Gnarl's source voice can become grave during major loss or existential danger. Establishing which REIGN subjects deserve that treatment will help keep humor from flattening serious lore.

---

# XIII. Questions that can safely remain UNKNOWN for now

These are not required before initial quest authorship unless a specific arc invokes them:

- exact number and identities of every intervening Overlord;
- exact calendar dates for ancient events;
- current Overlord bloodline if lineage is not part of the plot;
- full cosmological geometry linking Netherworld and Abysses;
- exact fate of every original-game supporting character;
- exact present location of every old franchise site;
- universal theology of every deity;
- complete economic institutions beyond the already-canonical use of Gold;
- complete laws of every civilization before its own questline is authored.

## Recommended interview order

To minimize rework, resolve questions in this order:

```text
1. Q-001 through Q-013: opening, player, Gnarl, Minions, Tower, first arc
2. Q-044 through Q-051: quest interaction model and tone
3. Q-014 through Q-020: historical memory and legacy-location policy
4. Q-021 through Q-032: one civilization at a time as its questline enters production
5. Q-033 through Q-043: cross-faction, religion, magic, and soul rules only where needed
```

This is enough to begin quest authorship without first writing an encyclopedic complete history of the entire world.
