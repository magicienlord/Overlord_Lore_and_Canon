# OVERLORD REIGN Mod Questline Assignment Continuation

Status: PROJECT DECISION MIRROR / INTERVIEW CONTINUATION

Date: 2026-09-13

Authority: records explicit Overlord decisions continuing `reference/33_REIGN_MOD_QUESTLINE_ASSIGNMENTS.md`. These entries are intended to be merged into the final consolidated assignment document when the interview closes.

## Mowzie's Mobs beyond Umvuthi / Umvuthana

Assignment: ABSORBED IN OTHER QUESTS / STANDALONE EPISODES / POPUP ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

Decision: no second general Mowzie's Mobs questline.

Umvuthi and the Umvuthana remain governed by their established civilization arc.

Other notable Mowzie's encounters, including major boss-type creatures, may be used by other quests that need suitable monsters, boss encounters, hunting targets, regional threats, or compact standalone episodes.

A future Monster-Hunter-style quest or similar authored content may draw from these creatures without turning the whole mod into one dedicated progression tree.

## Waystones

Assignment: NO QUEST INTEGRATION

Decision: no dedicated questline and no required popup acknowledgement.

Waystones remain gameplay infrastructure and must not be conflated with franchise Minion Gates or Netherworld transport lore.

## VillagersPlus + VillagerTradingPlus

Assignment: ABSORBED IN VILLAGER CIVILIZATION CONTENT

Decision: professions, workstations, trades, and provider opportunities from these mods belong inside the established Villager civilization framework.

They do not become separate factions or mod-specific questlines.

## Spice of Life: Carrot Edition

Assignment: POPUP MILESTONE TRACK / NO DEDICATED QUESTLINE

Decision: the system may receive character popup acknowledgements as the player advances toward the one-hundred-distinct-food milestone.

This should not become a separate formal questline.

A dedicated Minion cook NPC may be created to provide the recurring personality and commentary for this food-variety progression.

The Minion cook concept is PLANNED CHARACTER DESIGN pending final personnel implementation details.

Gluttony / Farmer's Spell remains the actual dedicated food-magic questline.

## Sophisticated Backpacks

Assignment: NO DEDICATED QUESTLINE

Decision: remain systemic inventory progression. Quests may use backpacks or upgrades as rewards where appropriate, but no narrative progression should be manufactured around the mod.

## Straw Statues

Assignment: NO QUEST INTEGRATION

Decision: personal decoration only. No questline, popup, or progression acknowledgement is required.

## Towns and Towers / Better Village / YUNG's structure overhauls / Luki's Strongholds and Woodland Mansions

Assignment: ABSORBED AS QUEST LOCATIONS

Decision: these are places where quests happen, not subjects requiring their own mod-specific questlines or introductory popups.

The Quest Maker may freely use suitable generated structures as authored locations, provided the specific quest remains consistent with the relevant civilization, lore, and progression state.

## RoadArchitect

Assignment: NO QUEST INTEGRATION / WORLD INFRASTRUCTURE

Decision: RoadArchitect supports the physical road network and world-building layer.

No dedicated questline or generic popup is needed merely because roads exist.

Individual roads, routes, bridges, or transport links may still become relevant to another authored quest when the story genuinely needs them.

## Interpretation rule carried forward

An affirmative answer from the Overlord confirms the proposal that was presented. It does not automatically promote a mod to a dedicated questline.

Dedicated questline status requires an explicit questline proposal or explicit Overlord decision.
