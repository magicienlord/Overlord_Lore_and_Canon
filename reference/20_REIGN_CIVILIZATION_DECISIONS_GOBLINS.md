# OVERLORD REIGN Goblin Civilization Decisions

Status: PROJECT DECISION MIRROR

Date: 2026-09-11

Authority: mirrors explicit OVERLORD REIGN decisions. Numbered project canon files remain authoritative.

## Quest Anchor and Leadership

Status: PLANNED / SOURCE-BACKED

The principal Goblin civilization quest anchor is a major Goblin Camp.

The civilization main quest begins through a designated Goblin leader using the installed `goblins_tyranny:leader_goblin` role. Ordinary Goblins and other Goblin camps do not automatically become civilization quest starters.

Quest-critical NPC protection applies to this designated leader under the global rule established for civilization main-quest NPCs.

## Baseline Behavior

Status: PLANNED

Goblin behavior outside the chosen anchor remains mod-native until authored quest state explicitly changes the chosen camp.

The existence of the Overlord does not globally rewrite all Goblin AI or allegiance.

## Subjugation

Status: PLANNED

The intended low-overhead SUBJUGATED package includes:

- safe use of the main Goblin Camp;
- access to merchants, shamans, engineers, craftsmen, tavern roles, and other appropriate Goblin services;
- improved prices where the existing mod mechanics can support them without a new continuous reputation system;
- tribute;
- additional Goblin sidequest access.

No simulated Goblin army, worker economy, or production simulation is required unless an existing mechanic provides useful functionality essentially for free.

## Local Scope

Status: PLANNED

Subjugating the chosen Goblin Camp does not make every Goblin generated elsewhere a subject of the Overlord.

Other Goblin camps and groups remain independent unless separately affected by authored quests.

A destructive branch may destroy the principal Goblin Camp, remove its providers/services, and permanently alter that anchor while Goblins continue to exist elsewhere in the world.

## Goblin View of Minions

Status: CANON CULTURAL BELIEF / BIOLOGICAL TRUTH UNKNOWN

Goblins and Minions view one another as something like distant cousins.

This is a cultural interpretation, not an established biological or historical fact.

No shared ancestry is canonized. The actual relationship, if any, remains UNKNOWN.

This perception may be used in Goblin/Minion dialogue and quest characterization without treating Goblins and Minions as the same people.
