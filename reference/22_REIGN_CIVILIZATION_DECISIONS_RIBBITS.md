# OVERLORD REIGN Ribbit Civilization Decisions

Status: PROJECT DECISION MIRROR

Date: 2026-09-11

Authority: mirrors explicit OVERLORD REIGN decisions. Numbered project canon files remain authoritative.

## Quest anchor and political scope

Status: CANON DESIGN / PLANNED IMPLEMENTATION

The Ribbit civilization main quest is anchored to one designated Ribbit Village.

That village is one independent local community rather than the capital of every Ribbit in the world.

The civilization main quest begins through one protected, explicitly marked Gardener assigned the local role of Elder. This is a political/social quest role layered onto the existing Gardener type rather than a new mob class.

Ordinary Ribbit Gardeners and Ribbit Villages elsewhere do not become civilization quest starters merely through player interaction.

## Baseline behavior

Status: CANON DESIGN

Ribbits are peaceful toward everyone and everything by default.

REIGN preserves their mod-native friendly behavior until explicit player actions and authored quests create a different local outcome.

The current Overlord's existence does not make Ribbits suspicious or hostile by default.

## Subjugation

Status: CANON DESIGN / PLANNED IMPLEMENTATION

Subjugating the canonical Ribbit Village affects that village rather than every Ribbit in the world.

The low-overhead benefit set is:

- safe village access;
- Merchant and Fisherman trade benefits where existing mechanics allow it;
- Sorcerer buffs or services;
- tribute;
- expanded Ribbit sidequests;
- continued access to the village's agricultural and local service infrastructure.

No separate Ribbit army, automated labor system, or simulated production economy is required.

## Destruction

Status: CANON DESIGN

Destroying the canonical Ribbit Village is a local anchor outcome.

Its providers, services, peaceful quest pools, and local settlement state may change permanently, while Ribbits elsewhere remain extant.

## Tone and dialogue

Status: CANON PRESENTATION RULE

Ribbit writing should preserve the source mod's cozy, musical, cheerful, and slightly silly character.

The Overlord tone comes from the contrast between that harmless culture and the player's ability to dominate, exploit, or destroy it.

Do not rewrite Ribbits into grim swamp warriors merely to make their questline feel more serious. Their innocence and cheerfulness are part of what makes Overlord-style cruelty, opportunism, or reluctant usefulness darkly comedic.
