# OVERLORD REIGN Final Mod Questline Assignments

Status: AUTHORITATIVE PROJECT DECISION LEDGER

Date finalized: 2026-09-13

Authority: this file is the final result of the mod-coverage interview. It supersedes the in-progress assignment state in `reference/33_REIGN_MOD_QUESTLINE_ASSIGNMENTS.md` and the continuation record in `reference/35_REIGN_MOD_ASSIGNMENT_CONTINUATION.md` for the purpose of deciding which installed or planned content receives a dedicated questline, is absorbed elsewhere, receives popup acknowledgement, or receives no quest-facing treatment.

This file complements `reference/32_REIGN_QUESTLINE_COVERAGE_LEDGER.md` and `reference/34_REIGN_TOWER_RESTORATION_DECISIONS.md`.

## Governing rules

- A mod does not earn a dedicated questline merely because it has many mobs, advancements, structures, equipment items, or bosses.
- A credible climax or endpoint strengthens the case for a questline but is not sufficient by itself.
- Dedicated questlines are reserved for content important enough to the intended play experience and strong enough to benefit from authored progression.
- Popup acknowledgement should be sparse and eventful, not repetitive. Prefer first discoveries, major encounters, important acquisitions, real progression milestones, or later callbacks.
- When a system has a dedicated Dark Tower room, initial room restoration belongs to the overarching Tower Restoration questline. Deeper mastery remains in its native progression or dedicated questline where one exists.
- Worldgen, infrastructure, utility, decoration, storage, equipment, or navigation mods should normally remain invisible as mod identities and be used by other quests where useful.
- An affirmative interview answer accepted the proposal that was presented. It did not automatically promote a mod to a questline.

# 3. Tower-restoration absorptions

## Hot Iron
Assignment: ABSORBED IN TOWER RESTORATION.
The dedicated Tower forge-room installation is part of Tower Restoration. Later Hot Iron use is not a separate Tower questline.

## Storage systems / display systems
Assignment: ABSORBED IN TOWER STORAGE ROOM RESTORATION where useful.
Storage Drawers, display cases, display furniture, and similar systems may furnish or implement the Tower Storage Room. They do not receive standalone questlines.

## Weapon / armor / display systems
Assignment: ABSORBED IN TOWER ARMORY RESTORATION where useful.
General weapon, armor, rack, display, or storage systems may implement the Armory. The room restoration is the quest; collecting every item or mastering each equipment mod is not.

## Minion implementations
Assignment: ABSORBED IN MINION / TOWER PROGRESSION.
Overlord Brown Minion and any future successfully integrated traditional Minion types belong to Minion progression. Quest logic acknowledges actual implemented unlocks only. Do not invent separate Hive-restoration chains for gameplay types that do not exist.

# 7. Popup restraint

Popup acknowledgement is not ambient spam.

Prefer triggers such as:
- first discovery of a genuinely notable creature, structure, system, or relic;
- first defeat of a notable named or elite enemy;
- a real progression milestone;
- a contextually important acquisition;
- a later callback where the fact matters.

Avoid:
- every kill;
- every repeated structure;
- every ordinary item acquisition;
- one popup for every variant in a mob overhaul;
- repeatedly explaining an already-understood gameplay system.

Use the most appropriate surviving personnel voice. Mortis is preferable for selected death/undead observations, Quaver for musical/court matters, and Gnarl remains the principal strategic commentator.

# 8. Quest Maker completion rule

Before Overlord Quests is considered content-complete, the Quest Maker must reconcile implementation against this final ledger and `reference/32_REIGN_QUESTLINE_COVERAGE_LEDGER.md`.

A reviewed mod may be absent from the quest log because its final assignment is ambient, systemic, popup-only, or absorbed elsewhere. That is intentional and must not be 'corrected' by inventing extra mod-specific questlines.

Likewise, content explicitly assigned a dedicated questline must not be silently downgraded to a generic popup or incidental objective.

Dark Tower room ownership is governed by `reference/34_REIGN_TOWER_RESTORATION_DECISIONS.md` and takes precedence over creating separate room-installation questlines.
