# OVERLORD REIGN Dwarven Civilization Decisions

Status: PROJECT DECISION MIRROR

Date: 2026-09-11

Authority: mirrors explicit OVERLORD REIGN decisions. Numbered project canon files remain authoritative.

## Historical continuity

Status: CANON / REIGN ADAPTATION

The present Dwarven condition results from Imperial anti-magic persecution during the Glorious Empire's ascendancy.

The franchise corpus directly establishes the Empire's persecution of magical beings, but it does not explicitly state that Dwarves are a magical race. OVERLORD REIGN therefore makes the additional canon decision that Dwarves are a magical people and were persecuted by the Empire on that basis, alongside Elves and other magical populations.

That persecution destroyed or suppressed the visible continuity of the old Golden Hills state. Survivors withdrew into reduced, isolated underground communities. Most political institutions and historical records were lost, scattered, destroyed, or became inaccessible.

The present Golden Hills / Golden Halls quest anchor is therefore a successor hold rather than a restored version of Goldo's kingdom.

## Government and quest entry

Status: CANON DESIGN / PLANNED IMPLEMENTATION

The Golden Hills successor hold is led by a local Forge-Thane.

Forge-Thane is a political title layered onto an existing Dwarven Forger NPC rather than a new entity class or profession.

The civilization main quest begins through this designated Forge-Thane. Ordinary Dwarves elsewhere must not become civilization quest starters through arbitrary interaction.

One designated quest NPC retains the surviving serious records of the Dwarven glory days. Ordinary Dwarves do not possess complete institutional memory of Goldo, the old Golden Hills state, or the old Overlords.

## Economy

Status: CANON DESIGN / PLANNED IMPLEMENTATION

The installed Dwarven Forge mod's amethyst-centered trading economy is preserved.

In-universe, amethyst became an entrenched practical internal exchange material during centuries of underground isolation and reduced contact with the old Golden Hills economy.

Gold remains culturally important as prestige, heritage, treasure, craftsmanship, and ancestral fixation. REIGN's general Gold currency may still appear in tribute and cross-cultural exchange.

Dwarven subjugation discounts should reuse inherited vanilla Villager price/reputation mechanics if technical testing confirms that the Dwarf entity responds to them correctly. A separate continuous pricing simulation should not be added unless necessary.

## Subjugation and destruction

Status: CANON DESIGN / PLANNED IMPLEMENTATION

Dwarven subjugation remains mechanically lightweight. Expected benefits include safe access, improved or unlocked trades, access to Dwarven runes/equipment, tribute, and quest/service access.

No persistent automated mining, labor, or worker simulation is required.

A destructive resolution applies to the Golden Hills quest anchor, not to the Dwarven species globally. The anchor can lose its providers, peaceful trade, and sidequest pools while ordinary Dwarves elsewhere remain extant.

## Quest-critical NPC protection

Status: PLANNED GLOBAL QUEST RULE

Civilization main-quest NPCs must be protected from accidental or environmental death so questlines cannot be permanently broken by unrelated world events.

They remain vulnerable to deliberate player-caused violence when the relevant authored quest/state permits that outcome.

This rule is global across civilizations, not Dwarf-specific.
