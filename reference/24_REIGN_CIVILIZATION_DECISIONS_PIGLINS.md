# OVERLORD REIGN Piglin Civilization Decisions

Status: PROJECT DECISION MIRROR

Date: 2026-09-11

Authority: mirrors explicit OVERLORD REIGN decisions. Numbered project canon files remain authoritative.

## Origin

Status: CANON

Piglins ultimately descend from an ancient union between a Minion and an ordinary pig.

This is not merely an in-universe rumor or joke. It is true in OVERLORD REIGN.

Gnarl regards this origin as a major source of embarrassment and shame. The subject can support Overlord-style comedy precisely because the underlying fact is real while Gnarl would strongly prefer that it not be discussed.

## Netherworld history

Status: CANON

Piglins existed in the Netherworld before the current reign, but long-term Minion proliferation and territorial dominance kept Piglin populations comparatively constrained.

During the Silence, Minion numbers collapsed and normal Minion spawning ceased without a Master. Piglins consequently expanded into abandoned or weakly held Netherworld territory and developed into a much more visible civilization.

This explains why Piglins can now support a major Netherworld civilization questline without contradicting earlier Minion dominance.

## Quest anchor and leadership

Status: CANON DESIGN / PLANNED IMPLEMENTATION

The Piglin civilization main quest is anchored to one designated Nether Village.

The main quest begins through one protected, explicitly marked Piglin Brute assigned the social title `Chieftain`.

This title is layered onto the existing Piglin Brute entity rather than implemented as a new mob class.

Ordinary Piglin Brutes, Piglins, Bastion populations, and other Nether settlements do not automatically become civilization quest starters through arbitrary player interaction.

The chosen Nether Village is one local Piglin polity rather than the capital of every Piglin in the Netherworld.

## Baseline behavior

Status: CANON DESIGN

Vanilla Piglin behavior is preserved outside the authored quest-state exceptions.

Ordinary Piglins may initially tolerate the Overlord under normal vanilla gold-armor rules. Piglin Brutes remain naturally hostile where vanilla behavior requires it, making the Chieftain and the village's political submission a distinct problem rather than globally neutralizing the species.

The use of gold armor for peaceful initial access is treated as an organic Minecraft mechanic, not an invented quest-only condition.

## Gold culture

Status: CANON DESIGN

Piglins retain vanilla-level obsession with gold.

Among current REIGN civilizations, Piglins take the economic fixation on gold furthest.

This does not replace the Dwarven relationship with gold. Dwarves retain gold as heritage, prestige, craftsmanship, treasure, and ancestral fixation, while Piglins are the more extreme everyday economic and behavioral gold-obsessed culture.

## Subjugation

Status: CANON DESIGN / PLANNED IMPLEMENTATION

Subjugating the canonical Nether Village affects that village rather than every Piglin in the Netherworld.

The village's authored Piglin and Piglin Brute population becomes Overlord-aligned and ceases attacking the Overlord regardless of normal gold-armor conditions.

Within the subjugated village, the normal vanilla anger rules for opening Piglin containers, looting chests, or mining local gold are suspended for the Overlord. Submission must permit the Overlord to treat the village's resources as subject property without being attacked for exercising that authority.

Wild Piglins, Piglin Brutes, Bastion populations, and other Piglin settlements retain vanilla behavior unless separately affected by authored quests.

The low-overhead benefit set includes:

- safe Nether Village access;
- unrestricted access to the subjugated village's containers and local gold without Piglin retaliation;
- bartering and Piglin services;
- improved or expanded Piglin quest rewards where supported;
- tribute;
- access to Nether resources and local quest pools.

No global Piglin AI rewrite or persistent Piglin empire simulation is required.

## Piglin and Minion relationship

Status: CANON CULTURAL RULE

Piglins and Minions recognize one another as unusually close Netherworld cousin-peoples.

Minions do not regard Piglins simply as inferior servants or vermin. Their much closer ancestral connection gives the relationship a warmer and more familial quality than Minions' cultural cousin-language toward unrelated goblinoid peoples.

This does not make Piglins members of the four Minion tribes. They remain a distinct civilization with their own culture, behavior, and political communities.

Subjugation restores the Overlord's political supremacy in the local Piglin settlement without culturally converting Piglins into Minions.

## Destruction

Status: CANON DESIGN

Destroying the canonical Nether Village is a local anchor outcome.

Its providers, services, peaceful quest pools, and local population may be destroyed or permanently altered, while Piglins remain widespread elsewhere in the Netherworld.
