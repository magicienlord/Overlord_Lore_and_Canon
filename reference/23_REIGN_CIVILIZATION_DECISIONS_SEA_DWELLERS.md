# OVERLORD REIGN Sea Dweller Civilization Decisions

Status: PROJECT DECISION MIRROR

Date: 2026-09-11

Authority: mirrors explicit OVERLORD REIGN decisions. Numbered project canon files remain authoritative.

## Quest anchor and political scope

Status: CANON DESIGN / PLANNED IMPLEMENTATION

The Sea Dweller civilization main quest is anchored to one designated Sea Village.

That village is one independent local community rather than the capital of every Sea Dweller in the world.

The civilization main quest begins through one protected, explicitly marked senior Sea Dweller trader assigned the social title `Sea Elder`.

`Sea Elder` is a quest/social role layered onto an existing Sea Dweller entity rather than a new mob class.

Ordinary Sea Villages and Sea Dwellers elsewhere do not become civilization quest starters merely through player interaction.

## Baseline behavior

Status: CANON DESIGN

Sea Dwellers retain their mod-native peaceful and trading behavior until explicit player actions and authored quests change the designated anchor's state.

The current Overlord's existence does not globally alter Sea Dweller allegiance or AI.

## Subjugation

Status: CANON DESIGN / PLANNED IMPLEMENTATION

Subjugating the canonical Sea Village affects that village rather than every Sea Dweller in the world.

The low-overhead benefit set is:

- safe village access;
- improved or expanded access to native Sea Dweller traders where existing mechanics allow it;
- underwater equipment and resources;
- Aquamarine-related commerce where supported by the installed version;
- tribute;
- expanded Sea Dweller sidequests;
- continued use of the village's native services and underwater infrastructure.

No separate naval army, automated underwater labor system, or new production simulation is required.

## Ocean Dragon progression

Status: CANON DESIGN / PLANNED QUEST INTEGRATION

The native Ocean Dragon questline remains its own progression path.

OVERLORD Quests should point the player toward that native questline rather than replace or duplicate it.

Questlog remains authoritative for the OVERLORD REIGN story state around that progression. It should track the relevant Ocean Dragon milestones so the civilization and main-story quests can react to completion without taking ownership of the Ocean Dragon mod's internal mechanics.

The Ocean Dragon is politically separate from the Sea Village. Subjugating the village does not subjugate, ally, or otherwise change the Dragon automatically.

## Wild and non-anchor Sea Dwellers

Status: CANON DESIGN

Naturally occurring Sea Dwellers and other Sea Villages outside the designated anchor are not globally changed by the anchor's disposition.

They retain their ordinary mod-native behavior unless separately addressed by authored quests.

## Destruction

Status: CANON DESIGN

Destroying the canonical Sea Village is a local anchor outcome.

Its providers, peaceful services, and local quest pools may disappear or change permanently, while Sea Dwellers and other Sea Villages elsewhere remain extant.
