# OVERLORD REIGN Quest-Lore Interview Decisions

Status: PROJECT DECISION MIRROR

Date opened: 2026-09-11

Authority: this file mirrors explicit OVERLORD REIGN decisions made during the quest-lore interview. The numbered canonical project files remain the authoritative REIGN canon database.

## Opening State and Current Overlord

### Q-001: How does the current Overlord enter the story?

Status: `CANON`

During the final day of the Silence, the future Overlord hears an unspoken, unreadable call drawing him to an unexplained piece of black Netherworld rock.

When he touches the rock, the Netherworld senses his potential and chooses him. The new Dark Tower immediately erupts, grows, and sprawls upward from beneath him.

The current Overlord is anointed by the Netherworld itself at the summit of the newly manifested Tower. Gameplay begins there.

The active emergence of the new Overlord after this event brings the Silence to its end.

### Q-002: What does the current Overlord remember?

Status: `CANON / INTENTIONALLY UNKNOWN`

His own origin is unknown even to himself.

He begins without reliable knowledge of his previous life, ancestry, or why the Netherworld called specifically to him.

### Q-003: How is the current Overlord recognized as legitimate?

Status: `CANON`

The Netherworld's choice is the sole initial basis of his anointment and legitimacy.

There is no initial bloodline proof, predecessor designation, Minion vote, or additional ceremonial test.

Gnarl and the Minions may still have questions about what sort of Master they have received. His subsequent actions, rather than another formal proof, answer those questions and their long-standing hopes for a new Overlord.

### Q-004: Is the current Overlord's ancestry relevant?

Status: `INTENTIONALLY UNKNOWN`

A hereditary relationship to earlier Overlords may be possible, but it is not established.

The question is allowed to remain hanging indefinitely and need never receive a definitive answer.

This means ancestry must not become a mandatory explanation of his legitimacy. The Netherworld chose him regardless of whether a hidden blood relationship exists.

### Q-005: Does the current Overlord speak?

Status: `CANON`

The current Overlord is a silent protagonist, following the presentation tradition of his predecessors.

Player intent is expressed through actions, quest decisions, and persistent world consequences rather than authored spoken dialogue.

For quest writing, NPCs and Gnarl may react to what the Overlord does or selects, but the system should not require ordinary authored protagonist speech.
